This spell functions exactly as _Feign Death_ save that it may be cast on unwilling targets and during the spell the target is entirely unconscious and unaware of their surroundings. An unwilling target must be touched with a successful combat roll and is allowed a CON-modified saving throw to resist. The target's body and any possessions on it will not decay, diminish, or fade in any way. The caster may set the spell to end at a predetermined time earlier than its duration or to end when a specific set of actions occur to the target (a brass bell is rung in their presence, they're kissed by a prince, etc. ).
