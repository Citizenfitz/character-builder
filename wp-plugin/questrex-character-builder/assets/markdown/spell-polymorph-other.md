This spell transforms one creature into another creature no smaller than a mouse nor larger than an elephant. The target is allowed a WIS-modified saving throw to negate the effect. The new creature cannot be:

- One with more than 1.5 times the caster's level in hit dice
- Non-corporeal (a ghost, a mist-creature, etc.)
- An exact duplicate of another creature (a dog can be turned into a man but not into a specific man).

The polymorphed creature has:

- The attributes (including mental ones), hit points, and abilities of the new form
- Physical limitations of the new form (a sorceress turned into a frog can't cast spells)
- Their gear melded into the new form and thus unusable
- Their original personality

The spell lasts until dispelled or the creature is reduced to 0 hit points. If the transformed creature's original form had more hit points than the polymorphed one it reverts back alive with its remaining hit points intact. If its original form had few hit points, it reverts back dead. The material component is a caterpillar cocoon.
