This spell conjures a flaming sphere that streaks from the caster's outstretched hand to violently detonate upon impact with a low roar. All creatures within the spell's blast radius suffer 1d6 points of damage per level of the caster or ½ that with a successful saving throw. The heat of a _Fireball_ is great enough to alight combustibles and melt softer metals, but it has little concussive effect and will generally conform to the space in which it discharges. The material component is a pea-sized ball of bat guano and sulfur.
