_Sticks to Snakes_ transforms 1 stick into a snake for every level of the caster. The snakes will follow the caster's mental commands. The sticks:

- Must be at least as large as an arrow
- Cannot be magical in nature (e.g. wands, magic spears, etc.)
- Have a chance of becoming a venomous snake equal to 5% per level of caster

**_Reverse: Snakes to Sticks -_** The reverse will turn 1 snake into a plain stick for the duration of the spell. Each serpent is allowed a WIS-modified saving throw to avoid the effect.
