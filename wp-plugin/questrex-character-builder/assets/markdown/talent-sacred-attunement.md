**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
The character is deeply attuned to their deity’s element or sphere of influence, chosen narrowly when this talent is taken and fixed thereafter. This attunement grants:

- **Reaction**: Bonus to reaction rolls with creatures directly tied to the element/sphere (see table).
- **Resistance**: With a successful talent roll (**DC 10**), the character gains resistance (see Conditions, q.v.) to harmful effects of the element/sphere.
- **Unhindered**: With a successful talent roll (**DC 10**), the character moves freely through environmental conditions or obstacles tied to the element/sphere.

#### ⠀Examples of Common Elements/Spheres

- **Fire**: _Reaction bonus_ with fire elementals; _resistance_ to fire effects (e.g., fireballs).
- **Water**: _Reaction bonus_ with water creatures (e.g., mermaids, tritons); _unhindered_ when swimming in rough seas or riptides.
- **Air**: _Reaction bonus_ with air elementals; _resistance_ to air effects (e.g., hurricane, lightning); _unhindered_ by strong winds (e.g., gales).
- **Earth**: _Reaction bonus_ with earth elementals; _resistance_ to earth effects (e.g., cave-ins); _unhindered_ by rugged, craggy terrain.
- **Spiders**: _Reaction bonus_ with arachnids; _resistance_ to spider effects (e.g., poisons, _Web_ spells); _unhindered_ by non-magical webbing.
- **Ice**: _Reaction bonus_ with cold creatures (e.g., polar bears, yetis); _resistance_ to ice effects (e.g., frostbite); _unhindered_ by deep snow or icy conditions.
- **Darkness**: _Reaction bonus_ with darkness creatures (e.g., shadows, shades); _unhindered_ by darkness (e.g., Darksight 15 ft).
- **Death**: _Reaction bonus_ with undead; _resistance_ to death effects (e.g., _Vampiric Grasp_ death-magic attacks, special attacks from undead)
- **Woodlands**: _Reaction bonus_ with woodland creatures (e.g., dryads, satyrs); _resistance_ to woodland effects (e.g., charms); _unhindered_ by dense briars or overgrowth.
