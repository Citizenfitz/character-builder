This spell enables the caster to read the surface thoughts of other creatures after concentration on them for a full round. The material component is a bit of copper wire.

- Undead and mindless creatures are not affected
- The caster will understand the thoughts regardless of the target's language
- If targeting a group (say whatever creatures are behind a door) the caster will get a sense of how many there are but must spend a round focusing on a specific creature to read their thoughts.
- The spell is blocked by 2 feet of stone, two inches of metal, or a thin foil of gold or lead.

Targets with the _Psychic Sensitivity_ talent will automatically detect their mind being probed with _EPS_ on a DC 15 check and may then control what enters their surface thoughts with a WIS-based saving throw.
