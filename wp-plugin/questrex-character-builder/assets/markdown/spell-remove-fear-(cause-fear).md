This spell instills bravery in one creature as follows:

- Permanently removes all magical fear effects a creature is currently suffering
- Grants them advantage on saving throws vs fear for the spell's duration
- Grants them +3 on Morale rolls for the spell's duration.

**_Reverse: Cause Fear -_** This spell causes the victim to flee from the caster at maximum speed for the spell's duration. The target is allowed a saving throw to resist.
