This ability afflicts the target with a rigid paralysis that prevents all movement. _Mental Paralysis_ affects any intelligent creature (except undead or many mechanical intelligences) who's hit dice do not exceed twice the user's (i.e. a character with 5th level _Mental Paralysis_ can affect a creature with 10 or less hit dice). Paralyzed creatures may still use psionic abilities normally. Targets may make a WIS-based saving throw to avoid the effect.

- **x2 Stacking:** The power may target two creatures within range
- **x3 Stacking:** The power may target four creatures within range
