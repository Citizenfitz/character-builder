This spell causes up to 6 creatures in the area of effect to forget everything that has happened in the last 5 minutes per three levels of caster (5 minutes at 3rd level, 10 minutes at 6th, etc.). The caster may make targets forget less than the maximum time if he or she so wishes. A successful WIS-modified saving throw to resists the effect but it is harder if the caster is affecting fewer creatures:

- 1-2 creatures = targets save at -2
- 3-4 creatures = targets save at -1
- 5-6 creatures = targets saves normally

Memories can only be restored by _Heal, Restoration_, or _Limited Wish_ spells.
