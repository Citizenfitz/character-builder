The character is particularly attuned with their deity's elemental or sphere of influence. The exact element/sphere must be narrowly specified when this talent is taken and does not change. The character gains:

- +3 to reaction rolls on creatures directly associated with the element/sphere (+2 for adjacent, +1 for opposing)
- With a successful talent roll **(DC 10)** non-magical or low-level instances of the element/sphere will not affect or hinder the character.
- Against a magical or extreme instance of the element/sphere the character gains a +4 _(+2 for adjacent, +1 for opposing)_ bonus to all saving throws and any damage is reduced by 1 point per die rolled (minimum 1 point per die).

Examples of Common Elements/Spheres:

- **Fire**: +3 reaction from fire elementals; immunity to low-level fires (can walk across a bed of coals); +4 on saves and reduced damage vs fireballs or very intense fires.
- **Water**: +3 reaction from mermaids, tritons, etc.; able to swim easily in very rough storms; +4 saves and reduced damage vs water-based attacks.
- **Air**: +3 reaction from air elementals; may walk through strong winds without being buffeted; +4 on saves and reduced damage vs wind or lightning-based attacks or very intense winds such as tornadoes.
- **Earth\*\***:\*\* +3 reaction from earth elementals.; able to move through very craggy mountains at normal speed; +4 on saves and reduced damage vs earth-based attacks, cave-ins, landslides, etc
- **Spiders**: +3 reaction from all arachnids; able to walk through non-magical webbing without being entangled; +4 on saves and reduced damage vs spider-based poisons or web effects.
- **Ice**: +3 reaction from cold-inhabiting creatures (polar bears, yetis, etc.); able to stay in sub-zero temperatures without harm; +4 save and damage reduction vs cold-based attacks.
- **Darkness**: +3 reaction from darkness-based creatures (shadows, shades, etc. ); _Darksight_ for 30’; +4 save and damage reduction vs darkness-based attacks or effects.
- **Death**: +3 reaction from all undead; +4 save and damage reduction vs death-magic attacks or special attacks from undead.
- **Woodlands**: +3 reactions from magical woodland creatures (dryads, satyrs, etc.); able to walk through thick briars without being slowed or prickled; +4 on saves and reduced damage vs the charms or other special attacks of magical woodland creatures.
