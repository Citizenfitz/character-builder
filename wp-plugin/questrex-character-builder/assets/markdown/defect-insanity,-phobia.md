The character is unreasonably terrified of a certain situation, creature, or type of objects. When presented with the source of phobia they must make a WIS-modified saving throw to avoid the phobia's effect for 10 minutes. Roll d6 to determine their type of phobia. The GM is free to create new ones that will fit well into the game.

| D4 Roll | Phobia                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :-----: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  **1**  | **Acrophobia** - Character refuses to be more than 30 feet above solid ground and if forced to that height will do nothing but cling to the nearest object or person and scream.                                                 |
|  **2**  | **Agoraphobia** - Character refuses to be more than 200 feet from a wall or enclosed space. If they are further away they may do nothing but drop to the ground and crawl to the nearest wall or enclosed space while screaming. |
|  **3**  | **Creature** - Character refuses to be nearer than 100 feet to a specific type of creature (spiders, snakes, bears, robots, etc.) and will flee at top speed for 1d4 rounds while screaming if they are forced closer.           |
|  **4**  | **Hydrophobia** - Character refuses to enter or approach bodies of water large enough to swim in. If in such bodies they may do nothing but swim at top speed to the nearest land while screaming.                               |
|  **5**  | **Nyctophobia** - Character refuses to enter areas of complete darkness and if forced into them will stand frozen in place and scream uncontrollably.                                                                            |
|  **6**  | **Pyrophobia** - Character refuses to be nearer than 100 feet to fires larger than a single torch or smoke-filled areas and will flee at top speed for 1d4 rounds while screaming if they are forced closer.                     |
