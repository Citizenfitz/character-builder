**Modifier:** Charisma  
**Bonus:** none  
The character may perform music or oration that inspires nearby allies (within 120 feet). Subjects must be able to hear and see the character for the effects to apply. Inspiration has three effects:

- **The Quieting**: While performing, the character gains and grants allies a bonus on saving throws against **mind-influencing effects** (_Beguilement_, _Sermonize_, charm, fear, domination, suggestion, mental commands), but not against illusions or direct mind attacks (e.g., _Forget_, _Psionic Blast_). Even when no such effects are present, they gain and grant allies a +2 bonus on morale checks, resisting intimidation, and fear-based natural effects.
- **Dispel Charm**: As an action, the character may target a single visible ally suffering from a mind-influencing effect as defined under The Quieting. The ally immediately makes a new saving throw with the character’s Quieting/Exhortation Bonus added. This ability may be used a number of times per day as shown on the Inspiration Talent Table.
- **Exhortation**: As an action, the character may exhort a single visible ally, granting the Quieting/Exhortation Bonus to any one roll of the ally's choice (attack, saving throw, or skill/talent roll). The bonus must be used within 5 minutes or it is lost. Exhortation may be used a number of times per day as shown on the Inspiration Talent Table.
