The character may perform music or oration that has inspirational effects on subjects nearby (within 120’). For this talent to work the subjects must be able to clearly hear and see the user. Characters gain additional effects as their talent level increases:

- 1st level = Counter Charm
- 2nd level = Inspiration
- 5th level = Exhortation
- 12th level = Remove Charm

#### Counter Charm

The character's music negates the effect of any music-based charm or fear effects (such as the Beguilement talent, song of sirens, drums of fear, etc.) within the radius of effect. Any characters already under the influence of such effects when the counter charm starts are allowed another saving throw with _Advantage_ to resist. This ability has no limit on usage.

#### Inspiration

The character may inspire greatness that will allow a single subject a bonus on any one roll of the subject's choice. The bonus to a roll must be made within 5 minutes of the performance and is equal to 1d4 + the performer's Charisma bonus. This ability may be used once per day per talent level.

#### Exhortation

With a successful talent roll **(DC 10)** the character may inspire all allies within range to make rolls for morale or against fear-based effects or spells with _advantage_. Allies already under the influence of fear-based effects are immediately allowed another saving throw with _advantage_ to resist. This ability has no limit on usage.

#### Remove Charm

The character may give a performance to remove charms and mind effects on a subject. The subject is allowed new saving throws at +4 to negate each effect afflicting them. Powerful mind effects which do not allow a saving throw cannot be removed by this ability. This ability may only be used once per day, cannot be used in combat or during a large commotion.
