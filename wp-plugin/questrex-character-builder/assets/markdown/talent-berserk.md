**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
Once per encounter, and with a successful talent roll **(DC 10)**, a character can enter a single-minded, enraged state which affords them:

- **Hit and Damage Bonus**: +2 to hit and damage with melee weapons.
- **Saving Throw Bonus**: +2 to all saving throws.
- **Strength Challenge Bonus**: +3 (+2 for adjacent, +1 for opposing) to any Strength-modified challenge rolls.

While in this state they're unable to take any actions except movement and melee attacks. They will unceasingly attack targets until the berserk state ends—first opponents and then comrades! A character may end their berserk by making a DC 10 Wisdom challenge roll or having complete solitude for 2d6 minutes. Comrades may assist in this by making a successful DC 10 Charisma challenge roll to implore the berserker to stop. If successful, the berserk character may make an additional DC 5 Wisdom challenge roll to end the berserk.
