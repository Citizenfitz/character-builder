This spell functions as a _Raise Dead_ spell with the following exceptions:

- The subject is immediately restored to full health and vigor.
- Any lost or damage body parts are restored
- The subject can be dead for 10 years per level of caster
- Only the scantest of remains of the subject are needed to resurrect them.

_Resurrection_ is exceedingly taxing on the caster and will leave them weak and bedridden for 1 day per level of the subject resurrected (no travel, combat, or spell casting, etc.).

**_Reverse: Annihilation -_** The reverse of this spell, Annihilation, causes the target to instantly die and turn to dust. It requires a successful hit in combat
