**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** special  
**Prerequisite:** Must be 1st level. Cannot have any other race talent.  
Half-elves are born of human and elven lineage, often strongly resembling their elven parentage but with greater height (around 5½ feet) and sturdier builds (approximately 150 lb).

- **Attributes:** +1 DEX (max 18)
- **Languages**: Common, Elvish.
- **Movement**: 30’.
- **Saving Throws**: +4 vs. ghoul paralyzation.
- **Darksight**: As the spell.
- **Perception Bonus**: +2 to all perception rolls and checks.
- **Size**: Medium.
