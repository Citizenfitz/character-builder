**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
The character's religious devotion sanctifies their body, granting resistance to disease and poison with a saving throw bonus (see table below). Furthermore, the bodies of deceased incorruptibles decompose at one-tenth the normal rate and will be shunned and not eaten by non-magical, non-intelligent animals.

##### Incorruptibility Levels

| Level | Disease and Poison Save Bonus |
| :---: | :---------------------------: |
|  1st  |              +3               |
|  4th  |              +4               |
|  8th  |              +5               |
| 12th  |              +8               |
