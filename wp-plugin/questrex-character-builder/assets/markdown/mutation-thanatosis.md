The character is able to directly modulate their life functions giving them:

- The ability to hold their breath for 10 minutes + CON mod.
- +3 on saving throws vs poison, disease, or death-effects
- The ability to enter into a cataleptic state indistinguishable from death identical to the spell _Feign Death_. The character may remain in this state for up to 8 hours during a 24-hour period.
