A character must have Wizardry to take this talent. With a successful roll character can change parameters of a spell on-the-fly. For example, a spell's range could be halved but it's area of effect doubled. All changes must be approved by the GM and should generally involve trading off one benefit for another. Radical shifting of a dweomer effect such as turning a cold spell into a fire spell will greatly increase the DC roll.

If the refashioning roll fails, the spell fizzles without result. On a significant failure the GM may have the spell function with contrary or calamitous results for the caster.

| ACTION                                                                                               | DC  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-: |
| **Cosmetic Change** - (i.e. the color of a fireball)                                                 |  5  |
| **Change** - (double range and half area of effect; increase casting time and double duration, etc.) | 15  |
| **Major Change** - (i.e. fire spell to cold spell; Wall if Ice to Wall of Mud, etc.)                 | 20  |
