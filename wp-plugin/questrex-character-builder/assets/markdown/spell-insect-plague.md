This spell summons a writhing mass of crawling, hopping, and flying insects that swarm about the caster's enemies. Creatures within the thick hoard:

- Suffer 1d4 hp of damage per round (no saving throw)
- Can only see 30' distance
- Must make a WIS-modified saving throw to remain in the swarm. Failure indicates they flee at top speed.
  The material component is a bit of smeared fat.
