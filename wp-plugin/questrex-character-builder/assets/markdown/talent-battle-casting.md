**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
The character may wear armor and still cast _Wizardry_ spells whilst wearing it. They still may not cast spells with somatic components when bound or restrained. The character may also use all weapons (swords, lances, etc.).

##### Battle Casting Levels

| Level |          Proficiency           |
| :---: | :----------------------------: |
|  1st  |  Light armor (e.g., leather)   |
|  4th  | Medium armor (e.g., chainmail) |
|  8th  |   Heavy armor (e.g., plate)    |
| 12th  |     Heavy armor and shield     |
