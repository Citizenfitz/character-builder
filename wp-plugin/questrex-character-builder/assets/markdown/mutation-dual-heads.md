The character possesses two fully-functional heads and can perform such feats as holding two different conversations at once or having one head keeping watch while the other one sleeps. The character also gains:

- +2 on all saving throws vs mental attacks
- +2 on all perception rolls
- Only surprised on a 1 on 6
- _Advantage_ on **saving throws vs blinding attacks** such as Bioluminescent (each d20 roll is for one head so it is possible one is blinded and not the other).
- _Disadvantage_ on **saving throws vs other gaze attacks** (like petrification) where one head is likely to succumb to the attack even if the other avoids it.
