This spell heals 1d8+1 hit points of damage suffered by a living creature. It cannot raise their hit points above the creature's normal maximum. Note: casting _Cure Light Wounds_ on undead creatures will inflict 1d8+1 damage upon them.

**_Reverse: Cause Light Wounds -_** This spell inflicts 1d8+1 damage on a living creature. The caster must successfully touch the creatures with a Combat Talent roll.
