**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** +1 to Wisdom or Charisma. Neither can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
**Prerequisite:** 9th level or higher  
The character may establish a stronghold or organization and will attract followers appropriate to their class. The type of stronghold/organization and followers will vary greatly at the GM's discretion. A rogue might start a thieves guild and attract thieves. A priest might build a temple and attract acolytes and a congregation. A fighter might build a fortress and attract a retinue of men-at-arms.

- **Followers**: Gains a number of loyal followers (see chart below).
- **Generate Income**: Automatically generates 50 gp per level (e.g., taxes for fighters, tithes for priests, loot for rogues).
- **Rally Followers**: Followers and allies gain +3 morale and +1 against fear effects when the character is within eyesight.
- **Status**: The character, a prominent figure in the realm, gains advantage on reaction rolls when their status applies. For instance, as the lord of a new manor, they receive greater deference from other lords and commoners alike.
- **Summon Reinforcements**: Once per year may summon additional class-specific followers equal to their current follower count. These followers:
  - Can only be summoned for a GM-approved goal that fits the campaign (e.g., defend stronghold, execute heist, repel infidels, etc.).
  - Arrive in 1–4 d.
  - Serve for 1 wk then disband.

\*Adjust a character's followers based on their Charisma modifier, with a minimum of one die. For example, a rogue with 18 Charisma (+4) attracts 8d6 thieves, while one with 3 Charisma (-4) still gets 1d6.

##### Stronghold Follower Types

Followers are class-specific (1 HD, AC 13, ~1d6+2 or 1d8+2 attack unless noted). Veterans are 3 HD, AC 15, ~1d8+3 attack. CHA mod adjusts dice (e.g., +4d6 for CHA 18, -4d6 but min 1d6 for CHA 3).
