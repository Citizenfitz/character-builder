This hypnotic ability causes a creature to comply with a verbal suggestion from the user and is almost identical to the magic-user spell of the same name. Suicidal or obviously self destructive suggestions will be ignored unless they are very carefully worded. Creatures making a successful saving throw are unaffected but the GM can make the saving throw more difficult for particularly reasonable or convincing suggestions. _Suggestion_ does not impart any special language ability.

- **x2 Stacking:** The suggestions lasts 12 hours
- **x3 Stacking:** The suggestions lasts 24 hours
