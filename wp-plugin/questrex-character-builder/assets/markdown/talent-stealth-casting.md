**Modifier:** Dexterity  
**Bonus:** none  
**Prerequisite:** Prayers or Wizardry 1  
With this talent a character may furtively cast spells without onlookers realizing they are doing so (veiled incantations, whispered rites, etc.). This is handled as a contested challenge roll of the character’s _Stealth Casting_ vs. the onlooker’s perception (see Actions & Dice Rolls, q.v.). Failure means the spell proceeds but is noticed.
