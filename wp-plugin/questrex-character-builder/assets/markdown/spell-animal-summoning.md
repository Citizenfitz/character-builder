This spell calls up to 8 normal, non-magical animals to aid the caster. They will happily fight or perform other tasks as the caster directs. The animals are not magically transported to the caster and arrive via their own locomotion. Thus any animals summoned must be native and nearby. If the type of animal the caster requests is not available (GM's discretion) then the caster may twice more attempt to summon different animals. If all three attempts are unsuccessful the spell fails. The animals:

- Must be normal, non-magical
- Must be 4HD or less
- Arrive in 1-4 rounds
- Must be local to the area where the spell is cast
