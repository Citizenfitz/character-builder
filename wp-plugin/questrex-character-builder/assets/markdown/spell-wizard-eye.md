This spell creates a floating, invisible eye the caster can see through. The _Wizard Eye_ can see with normal sight and _Darksight_ (as the spell). The caster can direct the eye to move in any direction at 120' per round and can pass through holes as small as 1" wide. The material component is a bit of bat fur. Targets with the _Arcane Sensitivity_ or _Psychic Sensitivity_ talents will automatically detect a _Wizard Eye_ on a DC 15 check (a dread sense of being watched) and may then end the spell with a WIS-based saving throw.
