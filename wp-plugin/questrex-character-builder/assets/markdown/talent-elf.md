Elves stand about 5ft tall, and weigh around 120 lbs. They have delicate, beautiful features, slender builds, and long pointed ears. Elves live very long lives and retain their youthful vigor right up till death. The race has an affinity for magic and nature. Their personalities are often described as friendly, but ethereal and detached. Characteristics:

- **Attributes:** +1 Dexterity (max 18), -1 Constitution (min 3)
- **Languages:** Common, elvish [Common, Elvish, Goblinese, Orcish]
- **Movement:** 30’
- **Darksight** (as the spell)
- **Immunity:** Immune to paralyzation by ghouls.
- **Perception Bonus:** +2 on all perception rolls & checks
