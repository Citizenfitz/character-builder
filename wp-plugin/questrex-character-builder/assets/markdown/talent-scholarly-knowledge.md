**Modifier:** Intelligence  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
The character is well educated in mathematics, natural sciences, law, art, and other such matters. They are able to speak and write with eloquence and may choose additional languages to know (see levels table below). They will be competent as lawyers or jurists and well-versed in the protocols of government bodies or royal courts. With proper time, equipment, and a successful talent roll (usually **DC 15**, but varies greatly) they may perform such feats as:

- Translating an unknown language.
- Determining if a substance is poisonous.
- Determining if coinage or jewelry are fakes.
- Identifying the composition of a non-magical substance.
- Identifying non-magical plants and creatures.
- Making general meteorological predictions.
- Performing detailed research at libraries, courthouses, etc.

With a successful roll character can recall useful lore and legends on topics or discern the approximate meaning of written texts. For magical texts they will be able to understand the basic nature of text. At a talent level of 7 or higher they may read and use magical scrolls. Mildly esoteric lore had a **DC 15** with more obscure information increasing the difficulty class accordingly.
