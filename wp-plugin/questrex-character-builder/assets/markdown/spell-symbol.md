This spell enables the caster to scribe one of three glowing sigils that hang in the air and have a powerful effect upon any creature viewing them and failing a WIS-modified saving throw. The symbols and their effects are:

- **Pain** - Creatures are wracked with pain and make all attack and/or DEX-modified rolls with disadvantage. This effect lasts for 2d10 turns.
- **Persuasion** - Creatures become charmed as a C*harmed Person* spell and adopt the general inclinations of the caster. This effect lasts for 2d10 turns.
- **Hopelessness** - Creatures become despondent and surrender for 3d4 turns.
