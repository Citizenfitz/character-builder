_Mind Clouding_ causes a single creature or object no larger than an elephant to generate psychic emanations which renders it invisible to direct observation by intelligent creatures failing a WIS-based saving throw (excluding intelligent undead). Creatures making their saving throw are unaffected by the clouding. _Mind Clouding_ only prevents direct, not indirect observation. An object rendered invisible by it could still be viewed on a camera, in a mirror, and might even cast a visible shadow...

- **x2 Stacking:** May target three creatures or objects within range
- **x3 Stacking:** May target five creatures or objects within range
