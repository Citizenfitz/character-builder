**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
The character may assume the form of a creature or creatures closely associated with their religion, functioning as a Fauna Form spell without requiring casting. The creature type(s) must be carefully defined when the talent is chosen, must align with their religion, and never changes. Many religions have no close association with a specific creature, making this talent unsuitable for their adherents. The creature’s size is limited, per Fauna Form, to no smaller than a mouse and no larger than a hippopotamus. The range of creatures depends on the religion, for example:

- **Nature Worship (Druids)**: Various woodland creatures native to the druid’s protected area.
- **Snake Deity**: Ophidians of all types.
- **Cobra Deity**: Just cobras.
- **Spider Deity**: Spiders.
- **Hunter Deity**: Wolves or some similar pack-hunting animals associated with the god.
- **Sun Deity**: Talent not allowed unless the god is linked to a specific animal (e.g., eagles).
