Character may presenting their holy symbol in a fervent expression of faith and repel (and possibly destroy or dominate) undead and infernal creatures (demons, devils, etc.). Turn Undead:

- Takes 1 action to perform in combat (see Combat, q.v.)
- Requires the target creature(s) to be within eyesight and 120’ of character
- Can be applied to groups of dissimilar monsters en masse by having the character make a single roll and then applying it to each.

To use this talent, have the character roll a d20 and consult the chart below. Round undead hit dice UP so a 8+1HD monster is turned as a 9HD one.

- **T = Turn or Trusted** - creature is automatically turned if talent user is good or trusted if user is evil.
- **D = Destroyed or Dominated** - creature is automatically destroyed if talent user is good or dominated if user is evil.

- **Turned** undead will flee in terror from the character at maximum speed for at least 1 minute or until out of sight and not approach again for at least 1 hour. It may still fight back if cornered. Intelligent undead may, of course, still find ways to hector the character from afar.
- **Trusted** undead will follow the talent user’s mental commands for 1 hour per the users’ level with Turn Undead. Intelligent undead will act as if they’re under a Charm Person spell (q.v.) and mindless undead following all orders without question.
- **Destroyed** undead will instantly turn to dust.
- **Dominated** undead will be under the complete mental control of the talent user for 1 hour per the users’ level with Turn Undead.
