This spell creates a visual-only, apparitional illusion with no auditory, olfactory, or other sensory elements. The illusion can be of anything the caster wishes as long as it fits within the spell's 15 foot cube, and can be moved anywhere within the spell's range. It can generate light equal to a campfire, and complete darkness. The illusion can be manipulated by the caster during the spell or it can be pre-programmed to do a specific set of simple actions. The material component for this spell is a bit of fleece.
