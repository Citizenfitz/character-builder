This spell creates an intangible apparition illusion of the caster anywhere within the spell's range. This illusory duplicate is indistinguishable from the caster; includes visual, audible, and olfactory elements; and can be made to move and talk as the caster wishes. Additionally, the spell creates a special link whereby:

- The caster can see and hear everything as if they were in the image's location
- The caster's spells can be made to originate from the image
