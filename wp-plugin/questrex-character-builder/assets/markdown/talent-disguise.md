**Modifier:** Charisma  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Charisma, Wisdom, or Dexterity. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
A character can disguise themselves and impersonate others. The more radically they change, the more difficult the **DC**. Attempts to successfully impersonate a person well known to those viewing the character will prove to be exceedingly difficult, if not impossible! A natural 1 roll indicates the character thinks they are disguised but are, in fact, obviously misrepresenting their appearance. Characters in disguise will often present themselves in dark, misty, or distracting environments when viewers will have natural penalties to their perception rolls.
