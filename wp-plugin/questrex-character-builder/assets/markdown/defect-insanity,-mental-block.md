The character refuses to acknowledge the existence of a certain target type of creature or object. In casual situations the character will unknowingly avoid the target (with irrational excuses if needed). If attacked by, or forced to confront the target they may respond normally but will make ALL Challenge rolls at -3 until they are away from the target or it has been vanquished. Afterwards they will be completely convinced that the target was, in fact, something completely different.

Roll to determine the type of metal block the character has. If the type is something not existing in the game (i.e. undead in a purely science fiction world) then ignore and roll again.

|  **2**   | Avians                                                 |
