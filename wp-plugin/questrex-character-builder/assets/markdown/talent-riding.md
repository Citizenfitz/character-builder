The character is particularly skilled at riding and may mount their steed as an instant action and perform actions like archery or spell casting whilst riding without penalty. The riding talent may also be used to avoid being thrown or dislodged from a mount. Finally, it may be used to exhort a steed to faster than normal speeds for short periods. Riding usually refers to horses but any number of other creatures could be substituted. It is assumed that a rider trained in one sort of steed can quickly adjust to any other given reasonable time.
