This spell enables the caster to converse with plants within the spell's range.

- **Normal plants** can only communicate in the most rudimentary form but will help the caster if they're able (providing information on creatures who've passed by them, parting to allow easy passage, etc.)
- **Unintelligent, Monstrous plants** will regard the caster as friendly for the spell's duration, and will not attack. They may help the caster if properly persuaded.
- **Sentient plants** may be communicated with but the spell has no effect on their disposition towards the caster.
