_Ego Whip_ shocks a target's mind with pain, despondency, and a feeling of worthlessness, and inflicts 1d6+1 hit points of damage. Targets may make a WIS-based saving throw to avoid the power. Targets brought to zero or lower hit points by _Ego Whip_ must make a saving throw or be afflicted with **Psychic Wounding** (q.v.). Non-intelligent creatures, Intelligent undead and some mechanical intelligences are immune to _Ego Whip_.

- **x2 Stacking:** Ego Whip inflicts 2d6+2
- **x3 Stacking:** Ego Whip inflicts 3d6+3
