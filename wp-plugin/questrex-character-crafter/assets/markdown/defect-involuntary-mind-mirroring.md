The character's mind unwittingly absorbs and mimics the personality of those he or she is in close proximity to. Each day they are in the company of others they must make a WIS-modified saving throw. Failure indicates their alignment, personality, and goals in life have shifted to those of either the group as a whole (e.g., a band of cutthroat brigands) or the character with the strongest personality in the group (GM's call). The character's personality change will endure until it's changed again by exposure to a different person or group.
