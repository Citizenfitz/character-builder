This spell creates a shadow illusion of a monster or group of monsters. The monsters can be anything the caster imagines but all must be of the same kind and cannot exceed one hit die per level of caster (thus, a 10-level caster could create one 10HD monster or ten 1HD monsters). Monsters must be created within a 20ft cube but then can move anywhere within the spell's range.

Creatures attacked by the shadow monsters are allowed a saving throw to disbelieve. If they fail, the monsters, including any special attacks they may have, are 100% real to that creature.
If the creature successfully disbelieves, or against undead and unintelligent creatures, the shadow monsters:

- Have no special attacks
- Have 20% of their normal hit points
- Inflict only 20% of their normal damage
- Have an Armor Class of 0
- Have 20% of the real creature’s strength

In either case all shadow monsters take double damage from silver weapons. The monsters can also perform basic non-combat tasks (opening doors, carrying a torch, etc.) but perform these at 20% of their real-world counterpart's strength.
