This power erects a hemispherical translucent psionic shell that is centered on and moves with the user. Those within the tower gain +5 on all saving throws versus mind affecting effects. The tower also cancels the following effects within its area of effect:

- Psychic Lightning
- Ray of Enfeeblement
- Haste or Slow spells
- Hold spells
- Paralyzation effects of all types

Note that the tower also prevents those within its confines from using the aforementioned abilities as well. The tower has no effect on other spells or physical effects (arrows, fire, etc.).
