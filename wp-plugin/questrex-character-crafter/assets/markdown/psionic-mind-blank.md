_Mind Blank_ attempts to completely conceal the psyche affording the user +3 versus all mind affecting attacks and immunity from:

- _Ego Whip_
- ESP and all mind or emotion reading (but not _Aura Reading_)
- Remote viewing, scrying, _Mind Stalking_
- Spells, devices, or abilities that detect mental emanations, psionics, or intelligent creatures
- _Psychic Sensitivity_
