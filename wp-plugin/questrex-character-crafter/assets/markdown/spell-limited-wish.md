By means of this powerful magik the caster may cause a single, one-sentence utterance to become reality and create such effects as:

- Duplicate any 6th level or lower spell of a color the caster has a talent in
- Duplicate any 4th level or lower spell of any color
- Undo the deleterious effects of spells such as _Forget_, _Polymorph Other_, _Suspend Life_, etc.
- Create another effect of similar power with those listed above.

_Limited Wishes_ cannot provoke massive changes in reality and GMs must adjudicate their use with great care so as to not imbalance the game. A _Limited Wish_ always follows the exact wording of the caster and can prove disastrous for those who phrase them carelessly...
