**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** special  
**Prerequisite:** Must be 1st level. Cannot have any other race talent.  
Elves are graceful humanoids, standing about 5 feet tall and weighing approximately 120 lb. They possess delicate, beautiful features, slender builds, and long, pointed ears. With exceptionally long lifespans, elves retain their youthful vigor until death. The race has an affinity for magic and nature, and their personalities are often described as friendly yet ethereal and detached.

- **Attributes**: +1 DEX (max 18), -1 CON (min 3).
- **Languages**: Common, Elvish, Goblinese, Orcish.
- **Movement**: 30’.
- **Darksight**: As the spell.
- **Immunity**: Immune to paralyzation by ghouls.
- **Perception Bonus**: +2 to all perception rolls and checks.
- **Size**: Medium.
