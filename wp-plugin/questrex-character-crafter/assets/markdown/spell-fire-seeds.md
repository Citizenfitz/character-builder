_Fire Seeds_ transforms berries, acorns, pine cones, or other small seeds into incendiary missiles that explode upon impact or with a verbal command from the caster. The caster can create either 4 large fire seeds or 8 small ones. _Fire Seeds_ look no different than their unenchanted counterparts and remain potent for 10 minutes per level of caster.

- **Large Fire Seeds:** can be thrown up to 40 ft; inflict 2d8 points of damage; alight combustibles within 10' of target
- **Small Fire Seeds:** can be thrown up to 20ft; inflict 1d8 points of damage; ignite combustibles within 5 feet of target.
