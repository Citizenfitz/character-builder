This reversible spell either closes and seals or opens and unseals a single door, gate, chest, etc. Locked items can be opened without breaking the spell by the caster or any other character who has the Wizardry talent at three levels higher than the caster. (Thus, a character with 7[^th] level in Wizardry could open a door locked by a 4[^th] level caster). _Wizard Lock_ and _Wizard_ _Knock_ can be used to cancel each other out.
