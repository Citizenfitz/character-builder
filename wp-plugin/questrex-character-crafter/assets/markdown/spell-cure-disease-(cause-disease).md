This spell cures the target of any one disease (including magical ones).

**Reversible: Cause Disease -** The spell’s reverse requires the caster to touch the target and the target is allowed a CON-modified saving throw to resist. If they fail they are afflicted with a terrible wasting disease that saps their strength and causes:

- _Disadvantage_ on all rolls involving physical acts (combat, swimming, riding, etc,).
- Unable to be magically healed of hit point loss.
- Death within 3d6 days.
