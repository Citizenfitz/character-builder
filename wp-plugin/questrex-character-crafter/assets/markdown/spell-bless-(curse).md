Casting: 1 action School: Black, White Components: V,S,MDuration: 1 hour Range: 60'Save: neg. Target: 20' cube
_Bless_ (or _Curse_) may only be cast on creatures not already engaged in combat. It grants all allies with in the area of effect:

- +1 on combat rolls
- +1 on damage inflicted in combat
- +1 on morale rolls.

**_Reversible: Curse_** **-** The reverse of this spell causes enemies within the area of effect to suffer: -1 on attack rolls; -1 on any damage they inflict in combat; and -1 on morale rolls. Victims are allowed a WIS-modified saving throw to avoid the effect.
