This curse reduces the victim's intellect and personality to that of a moronic child. Their Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma scores drop to 3 and they cannot:

- Cast spells
- Use magical items
- Read
- Speak any except their primary language and that in only the most rudimentary manner.
- Perform any challenging mental tasks (mathematics, drawing, etc.)

Feeblemind can be removed with a _Cure Disease, Remove Curse, Heal, Restoration_, or _Limited Wish_ spell.
