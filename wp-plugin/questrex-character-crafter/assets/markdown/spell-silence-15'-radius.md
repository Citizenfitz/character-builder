This spell completely dampens all sounds originating within a 15' radius but has no effect on sounds originating from without. Thus, a character within it cannot make noise themselves by talking, walking, or dropping a bag of hammers, but they can hear noises made beyond the spell's effect. The spell:

- Blocks spells with verbal components from being cast within its affected area
- May be cast upon an area or a creature. A creature is allowed an INT or DEX-modified saving throw. If they are successful, the spell takes effect directly adjacent to them and they may move out of its area.
