The user can move an object with their mind at a rate of 60' per round. _Psychokinesis_:

- Only affects one object at a time.
- Has no action/reaction. A user psychokinetically grabbing a galloping horse is not dragged along with it.
- Can perform very delicate work (picking locks, sewing, etc.).

**Stacking:** The size or weight on an object that can be moved depends on the PSP expended per round:

**Inflicting damage:** Psychokinesis cannot directly inflict damage on a target but it can attack with weapons or hurled objects. Such attacks require standard Combat talent rolls and inflict normal weapon damage plus the STR bonus of the Psychokinesis. Psychokinesis does not afford any "to hit" bonuses for its strength.

**Grabbing targets:** Unwilling targets may attempt a contest roll of their strength versus that of the Psychokinesis each round to break free from the effect. Failure indicates they are restrained by the effect and can be moved at the user's leisure.

**Grabbing items from targets:** The user may grab items from a target with a successful Combat roll. If the target is holding the object her or she may resist with a contest roll of their strength versus that of the Psychokinesis each round to hold on to the object.
