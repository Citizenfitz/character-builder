This power creates a translucent, immobile wall of pure telekinetic force. The barrier can be of any rough shape up to 60 square feet in size. It can be created floating in mid-air but must be created within a vacant space. If the barrier is created where a creature exists it is allowed a saving throw to remain on the side of the barrier it wishes. If it fails the user decides which side it remains on.

_Mental Barrier_ prevents the passage of all creatures and physical and energy attacks. It does not stop gaze attacks, dazzle attacks, non-physical spells, or telepathic psionic abilities. It is immune to _Psychic Crush_. _Psychokinesis_ and _Psychic Crush_ cannot work on the other side of a _Mental Barrier_.

The barrier can absorb 20 hp of damage before collapsing. A user may continuously prevent the collapse of an already established barrier by expanding the PSP cost again to add 20 more hp to it.

- **x2 Stacking:** Barrier may be up to 120 square feet in size
- **x3 Stacking:** Barrier may be up to 240 square feet in size
