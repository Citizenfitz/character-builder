This power creates an intangible, invisible sensor through which the user can see and hear as if he or she were standing in its location. The sensor appears anywhere within the power's range and can be moved at 30' per round thereafter. The user may switch their senses between their body and the sensor by using an action but may not use both at the same time. _Clairvoyance_ is stymied by force fields, _Mental Barriers_, a thin sheet of metal, or stone more than 1' thick.
Targets with the _Arcane Sensitivity_ or _Psychic Sensitivity_ talents will automatically detect being viewed with _Clairvoyance_ on a DC 15 check (a dread sense of being watched) and may then end the power with a WIS-based saving throw. _Aura Reading_ being used on an area will also automatically detect the clairvoyance sensor.
