This spell allows the caster to transform themselves into any humanoid creature no more than 25% larger or smaller than their original form. The change is mostly cosmetic but can impart limited abilities as would be appropriate for the new form such as:

- Darksight (as the spell) but limited to 30'
- Natural weapons (claws, tusks, etc.) that inflict 1d6 damage
- Gills that allow breathing underwater
- Webbed limbs that increase swimming speed
- Wings that afford slow, clumsy gliding

Alter Self abilities should be significantly less effective than their real-world counterparts as the changes wrought by the spell are only semi-real. If the caster is rendered unconscious or reduced to 0 hit points they instantly revert to their true form. Alter Self can disguise creatures as specific other creatures (a specific king, a specific maiden, etc.) functioning as the Disguise Talent at a level equal to the caster's spell-casting level.
