With this spell the caster opens a two-way, trans-dimensional portal to another plane of existence of the caster's choosing. The gate appears as a glowing riff 5-20' in diameter through which the other plane is hazily visible. Creatures passing into the portal are transported to the other plane where an identical gate exists leading back.
Once the portal is established the caster may summon a specific creature from that plane to come through. Demons, elementals, or even gods may be summoned as long as the caster knows their true name. When the summoning is made:

- There is a 100% chance that _something_ will respond and come through the portal.
- Exceedingly powerful beings (gods, arch-devils, etc. ) will likely send a powerful representative in lieu of personally appearing.
- The response of the being that appears is entirely up to the GM and heavily dependent on the situation.

_Gate_ is a _very_ powerful spell that offers grand role-playing opportunities with incredibly high stakes. Players and GMs alike are advised to use it carefully...
