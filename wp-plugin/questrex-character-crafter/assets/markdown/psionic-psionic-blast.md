This much feared power unleashes a cone of visible psychic energy that overwhelms the victim's minds with a sense of shock and awe. Those within the cone must make a saving throw or be stunned for 1d6+1 rounds. Targets who fail their save with a natural one must make an additional saving throw or be afflicted with _Psychic Wounding_ (q.v.).

**Stunned targets** can't move, can barely speak, automatically fail STR and DEX-based saving throws, and are attacked with advantage.

_Psionic Blast_ has no effect on mindless or undead creatures and may not function on many mechanical intelligences.

- **x2 Stacking:** 50' cone and targets stunned for 2d6+2 rounds
- **x3 Stacking:** 70' cone and targets stunned for 3d6+3 rounds
