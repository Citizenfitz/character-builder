The user can read the aura of a single creature or object, or an area no larger than 30' x' 30'. If the target has an aura then it will appear as a scintillating halo of colors surrounding the target that is visible only to the user. Reading a target's aura will give the psychic three pieces of information:

1. Each aura is unique and the user will be able to recognize an aura they've seen before.
2. The target's alignment (items or places with auras may not have an alignment).
3. The current colors of the target's aura (see Aura Color Table, q.v.)
