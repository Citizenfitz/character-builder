This spell conjures an immobile, invisible wall of nearly indestructible force. The wall can be of any rough shape up to 20 square feet per level of caster in size. It can be created floating in mid-air but must be created within a vacant space. It is immune to spells and all physical attacks, and extends to the ethereal plane, but can be destroyed by a _Dispel Magic_ or _Disintegrate_ spell. If the wall is created where a creature exists it is allowed a saving throw to remain on the side of the wall it wishes. If it fails, the caster decides which side it remains on. The material component is a bit of diamond dust.
