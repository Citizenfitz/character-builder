**Modifier:** Intelligence  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Intelligence, Charisma, or Dexterity. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
Assassination is the skill of planning and executing a target's covert murder. It requires:

- Proximity (as close as the same town).
- Knowledge of the target's location.
- 1d4 days (rushing adds +5 DC).

⠀The talent functions differently for **nonsignificant** and **significant** targets:

- **Nonsignificant NPCs**: No encounter is played out. A successful roll eliminates the target. Failure means the target survives, and investigators may use Perception rolls against the assassin's failed attempt to uncover clues. A natural 1 indicates the plan has gone disastrously, forcing the assassin to fight their way out of a dire situation.
- **Significant PCs or NPCs** (player characters or key figures): The encounter must be played out. A successful roll grants a major advantage, such as:
  - Automatic surprise or one free round of action upon encountering the target.
  - Knowledge of a critical weakness in the target.
  - Weakened target defenses (e.g., guards distracted, escape routes blocked).
  - Collusion with one of the target's confederates.
