This spell causes a humanoid creature failing their saving throw to regard the caster as a trusted friend and ally to be defended and obeyed. The creature does not become an automaton, but will regard requests and suggestions from the caster in the most charitable light. The creature will ignore suicidal orders or ones against its alignment and/or inherent nature. If the caster attacks the creature the spell is instantly broken. Once the spell ends the creature is aware they were charmed. The creature is allowed additional saving throws to negate the effects of the spell based on their intelligence score:

- 3-8 - one save per month
- 9-12 - one save per week
- 13-18 - one save per day
- 19+ one save per hour
