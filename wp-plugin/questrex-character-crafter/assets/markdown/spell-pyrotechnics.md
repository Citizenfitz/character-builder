This spell transforms any fire into either a choking cloud of smoke, or an aerial display of fiery, blinding fireworks. In both cases, the fire is extinguished at the end of the spell.

**The choking cloud**

- Fills an area 100 times the size of the fire source
- Obscures all vision beyond 2 feet
- Last 1 minute per level of caster

**The fireworks**

- Fill an area 10 times the size of the fire source
- Blind all creatures within 120’ who have a direct view of them for the spell's duration
- Last one round per level of caster
