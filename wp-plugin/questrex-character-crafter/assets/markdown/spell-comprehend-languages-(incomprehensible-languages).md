This spell enables a caster to touch a single creature or text and then understand their language or that writing for the duration. The caster cannot speak or write these languages. The spell cannot translate magical or coded writings. This spell is reversible (_Incomprehensible Languages_) and can be cast on a single creature or written document causing the creature's speech to be unintelligible or the text to be illegible. Creatures are allowed a saving throw to resist the effect. _Comprehend/Incomprehensible Languages_ spells can be cast to cancel one another.
