This spell instantly shifts up to seven creatures to a different plane of existence. All creatures must be in physical contact with each other (e.g. holding hands). The caster may also use the spell on an unwilling target. They must successfully touch the target in combat and the target is allowed a WIS or INT modified saving throw to avoid the effect. The material component is a special tuning fork calibrated to the frequency of the plane the targets are to be sent to.
