This spell causes a fire source to become intensely hypnotic. Any creature viewing the fire must make a WIS-modified saving throw or become utterly transfixed upon the flames. Transfixed creatures:

- Will not move or take any action other than gaze into the flames
- Are susceptible to one 12-word or less suggestion from the caster. This suggestion works just like the spell _Suggestion_ and the creature is allowed a WIS-modified saving throw at -3 to avoid the suggestion.

The _Fire Charm_ is broken for any creature if it is attacked or its view of the fire is blocked. The material component is a small cloth of gossamer-thin silk.
