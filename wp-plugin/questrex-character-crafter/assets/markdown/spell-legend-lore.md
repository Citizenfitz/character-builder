By means of this spell a caster can gain knowledge about a legendary place, person or thing. The spell's revelations will always be cryptic in nature (riddles, anagrams, ciphers, signs, etc.). The casting time depends on the caster's physical closeness and/or personal knowledge of the target.

- The caster is close physically to target: 10-40 minutes
- The caster knows details about target: 1-10 days
- The caster knows only rumors about target: 2-12 weeks

During the casting the magician may take no actions besides eating and rest. The material components are a special incense that must be burned for the duration costing 10gp per day.
