This spell hurls a powerful thunderbolt which causes 1d6 of damage per level of caster to all creatures in its path or ½ that amount if they make a saving throw. The bolt is powerful enough to sunder doors, shatter 1 foot of stone, alight combustibles, and melt low-melting-point metals. The bolt can be either:

- A single bolt of 5 feet wide and 20 feet long per level of caster
- A forked bolt of 10 feet wide and 10 feet long per level of caster

A _Lightning Bolt_ can originate from anywhere within the spell’s range and need not come directly from the caster. The component is a bit of fur and a glass rod.
