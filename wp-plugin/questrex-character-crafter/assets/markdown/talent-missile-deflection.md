**Modifier:** Dexterity  
**Bonus:** none  
With a successful talent roll **(DC 15)** character can dodge or deflect a low-velocity missile directed at them. This includes arrows, spears, etc., but not firearms, spells, energy rays, or the like. Multiple missiles may be deflected in a round (each with a separate roll) but for each one beyond the first there is a +2 cumulative increase to the difficulty class.
