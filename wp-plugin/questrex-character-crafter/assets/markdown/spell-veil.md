Veil creates a phantasm illusion which changes the appearance of everything, and all creatures, within the spell's area of effect to whatever the caster desires. Creatures' changed appearance:

- Remains even if they leave the spell's area of effect.
- Ends if they're killed or rendered unconscious.

_Veil_ is similar to a combination of _Hallucinatory Terrain_ and _Visage_ spells, but as it is a phantasm creatures will act as if all changes are completely real unless they successfully disbelieve the illusion. _Veil_ can disguise creatures as specific other creatures (a specific king, a specific maiden, etc.) functioning like the Disguise Talent at a level equal to the caster's spell-casting level.
