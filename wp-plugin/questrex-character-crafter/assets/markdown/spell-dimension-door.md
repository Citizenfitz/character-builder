This spell causes a magical glowing "door" to open and close about the caster instantly transporting them to any spot within the spell's range. _Dimension Door_ is unfailingly accurate and the destination may be a place the caster can see, one they're familiar with, or specified with directions and distance (i.e. "20 feet down"). The caster may bring along one other creature weighing no more than 500lb. Unwilling creatures are allowed a saving throw to avoid the effect. If a solid object occupies the destination the spell fails and the caster and any creature traveling with them suffer 4d6 points of damage.
