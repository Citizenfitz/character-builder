For the duration of this spell the caster can transform themselves into any other creature as small as a mouse or as large as an elephant. Each change takes 1 full round of action. If the caster is knocked unconscious or reduced to zero hit points the spell instantly ends and they revert to their true form. If they are alive when the spell ends they recover 1d12 lost hit points.

The new form cannot be:

- One that has more hit dice than the caster has level
- Non-corporeal (a ghost, a mist-creature, etc.)
- An exact duplicate of a specific creature (an old wizard may turn himself into a fair maiden but not a specific fair maiden).

In the new form the caster:

- Retains all mental attributes and faculties.
- Retains their hit points.
- Gains the physical attributes, armor class, locomotion, and any non-magical physical abilities of the new form (e.g.

if transformed into a fish they can breath under water, but if transformed into a dragon they can't breath fire).

- May not, depending on the form, cast spells
- May not use any personal gear as it is transformed with them
