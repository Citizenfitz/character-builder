_Earthquake_ causes a brief, but violent seismic event to strike an area equal to 5' diameter per level of caster. The tremor is highly localized and will not propagate outside the spell's area of effect. Depending on the terrain where it is cast it will cause:

- Cliffs to crumble
- Caverns to collapse
- Trees to be uprooted
- Marshes or small bodies of water to drain off to form rough, muddy ground
- Buildings to be thrown down in rubble
- The ground to crack open with large fissures

Creatures caught in the spell's area must make a Wisdom-modified saving throw. Failure indicates they have died in falling debris or a crushing fissure. Success indicates they suffer 7d6 damage and require 1d20 minutes to extricate themselves from debris and/or a fissure.
