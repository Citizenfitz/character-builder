The character may assume the form of a creature or creatures closely associated with their religion. This ability functions exactly as a _Fauna Form_ spell but requires no casting to effect. The type of creature(s) the character can transform into must be carefully defined when the talent is chosen, must align with their religion, and never changes. Many religions have no close association with a specific creature making this talent unsuitable for their adherents. This ability can be used once per day for every three levels with the talent.
