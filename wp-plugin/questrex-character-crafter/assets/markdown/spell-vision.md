By means of this spell the caster is able to invoke a revelatory vision about any place, person, or thing of their choosing. For the spell to function the caster must sacrifice an item of at least 300gp value. To determine the result of the vision the caster rolls 2d6:

**2-5 -** The vision strikes monomania into the caster forcing them to take up a _Quest_ (as per the spell) of the GM's choosing. This _Quest_ will likely be related to the subject of the vision.
**6 - 11 -** The vision is vague and grants cryptic, incomplete knowledge of the subject.
**10-12 -** The vision is clear and grants detailed knowledge of the subject.
