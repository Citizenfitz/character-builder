This spell causes a magical slumber to overcome the creature(s) affected. The spell cannot affect undead or any creature that does not normally sleep. It can target either:

- One creature with 4+1 or less hit dice or
- 2d8 creatures with 4 HD or less

If targeting a group of mixed hit dice, the weakest are affected first. Creatures affected by sleep can be killed instantly with edged weapons or awoken with one round of slapping, shaking, etc. Material components are a bit of sand, or wool, or rose pedals.
