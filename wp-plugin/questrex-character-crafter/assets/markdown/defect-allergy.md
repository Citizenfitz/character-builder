Character is susceptible to substances or elements that are innocuous to everyone else. Contact with the substance will cause the character discomfort and 1d2 HP damage per round. Minute traces won’t tigger the effect but generally more than a bucket-full or prolonged contact with less will. Roll to determine the allergy.

| D10 Roll | Allergy                             |
| :------: | ----------------------------------- |
| **1-2**  | Metal                               |
| **3-4**  | Plastic                             |
| **5-6**  | Intense electric or magnetic fields |
| **7-8**  | Water (other than for drinking)     |
| **9-10** | Wood                                |
