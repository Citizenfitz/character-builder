This spell may be cast as an instant Combat Reaction even if the caster has already acted in this round. This spell causes naturally-falling creatures to become light as a feather and drift downwards at a leisurely rate of 60 feet per round (10 feet a second). The spell ends for a creature when they land and they suffer no damage if landing while under the spell's influence. The spell affects a number of creatures equal to 3 plus half the caster's level (i.e. 4 at 2nd level, 5 at 4th level, 6 at 6th level, etc.). The material component is a bit of down.
