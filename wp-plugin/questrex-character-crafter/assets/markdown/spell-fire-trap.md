_Fire Trap_ allows the caster to lace any closable object (e.g. door, book, coffin, et. al.) with a potent trap that explodes into a 5' radius ball of fire. The _Fire Trap_:

- Does NOT harm the item that was trapped.
- Inflicts 1d4+1 points of damage per level of caster. Creatures in the blast can make a DEX-modified saving throw to suffer ½ damage.
- Is not triggered by the caster. They may open the object without fear.
- Is difficult to disarm **(DC 25)** and failure triggers the trap.

The material component is a bit of saltpeter or sulphur.
