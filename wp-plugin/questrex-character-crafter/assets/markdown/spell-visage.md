This spell enables the caster to alter the appearance of one humanoid creature, including their equipment and clothing. The new form must be generally man-shaped and bipedal, and no more than 1' shorter or taller, but otherwise can take on any appearance. The changes are wholly illusory and will not stand up to physical inspection (e.g. a hat made with this spell will allow rain to pass right through it). Unwilling recipients of the spell are allowed a saving throw to negate the effect. Visage can disguise creatures as specific other creatures (a specific king, a specific maiden, etc.) functioning like the Disguise Talent at a level equal to the caster's spell-casting level.
