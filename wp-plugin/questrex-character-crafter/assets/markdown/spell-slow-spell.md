Within this spell's area of effect one creature per level of caster is slowed to half their normal speed. Affected creatures suffer:

- Armor Class and Combat Talent suffer -2
- _Slowed movement_ (see Movement section, q.v.)
- May move OR attack in combat but not both, and can never make more than one attack per round.
- _Disadvantage_ on rolls requiring speed.
- _Disadvantage_ on initiative rolls.

A _Slow_ spell is countered by a _Haste_ spell and vise versa. The material component is a bit of treacle or the shell of a snail.
