_Simulacrum_ creates a permanent shadow illusion duplicate of any other creature. The duplicate is created over a period of 8 hours and must incorporate some small bit of the original creature. It is most often formed from snow and ice but some versions of the spell utilize a large alchemical vat. The duplicate loyally serves the caster and obeys their verbal commands. The duplicate appears identical to the original and has their speech and personality but:

- Has only 50% of their hit points
- Has only 50% of their abilities or levels with talents.
- Has only a 30% chance of recalling any specific memory from the original
- Cannot increase in levels
- Cannot heal
- Cannot regain spells or special abilities used (e.g, a duplicated dragon could breathe fire thrice but never again)

A duplicate can be repaired and regain its special abilities by having _Simulacrum_ recast upon it which takes only 1 hour. A simulacrum brought to 0 hit points instantly reverts to its base chemicals and dissolves.
Note that if a _Reincarnate_ spell is cast upon a _Simulacrum_:

- It will gain a genuine life force
- It will be able to heal and regain special abilities normally
- Have its chance of recalling the original's memories increased to 75%
- It has a 25% chance of becoming independent minded with a new personality and no longer under the caster's control.

The caster will have no way of determining if this has occurred.
