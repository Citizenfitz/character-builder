This spell allows the caster to dispel a magical curse placed upon a creature, place, or object. It will not permanently disenchant cursed magical items but it will allow a character to be rid of them.
**_Reverse: Curse -_** _Curse_ can inflict a wide range of woeful effects upon targets. Targets are allowed a WIS-modified saving throw to resist. Example curses include:

- **Curse of Deafness** - As _Weak Ears_ disadvantage
- **Curse of Blindness** - As _Weak Eyes_ disadvantage
- **Curse of Ill Luck** -2 on all saving and reaction rolls, _disadvantage_ on all gambling rolls.
- **Curse of Boils** - unsightly boil reducing CHA by 4
- **Curse of Tremors** - constant shakes reduce DEX by 4
- **Curse of Wasting** - Character's STR reduced by 4
- **Curse of Infirmity** - lingering illness reduces CON by 4
- **Curse of Flies** - Swarms of flies gather wherever character goes. CHA reduced by 2; reactions at -2; many places

will refuse character entry (taverns, palaces, etc.)
Alternative curses should be of similar power to those listed above.
