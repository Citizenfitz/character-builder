This spell summons the life force of a recently deceased creature back as a new form. The caster must touch the corpse of the deceased and they must not have died more than one day per level of caster. Within 1d6 turns the new incarnation will appear.

The new incarnation will have a 75% chance of recalling any specific fact from their previous life and will retain much of their personality. The new form will tend to rhyme with the original creature's disposition and have the same gender. Thus, a boorish, overbearing man might be reincarnated as a swine, toad, or bull, whilst a gentle maiden might be reborn as a lamb, doe, or dainty poodle. To determine the general type of reincarnation that takes place roll d100:

| D100 Roll | Type                                                                                                      |
| :-------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **00-60** | Very similar to the original creature (a human to another humanoid, a horse to another pack animal, etc.) |
| **61-95** | Different but non-magical creature (a human to a badger, a horse to a man)                                |
| **96-00** | Wildly different magical creature (a human to a griffin, and dog to a dragon)                             |

One the general type has been determined, the GM may randomly determine the specific result by a method of their own choosing.
