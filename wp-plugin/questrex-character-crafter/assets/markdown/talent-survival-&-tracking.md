**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** +1 to Wisdom or Constitution. Neither can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
The character is an expert outdoorsman able to:

- **Survive in Wilderness**: Automatically find 1 day’s food/water for 6 people (1 hour in hospitable lands, 2 hours inhospitable, 4 hours desolate), build shelter for 6 (1 hour), predict general weather for 24 hours (e.g., rain likely), and avoid getting lost without a map.
- **Tracking**: Tracks creatures, identify them and details about them (e.g., how long ago they made the tracks, were they limping, etc.).
- **Conceal Tracks**: Conceal own tracks from other trackers (see Contested rolls, q.v.).
- **Identify Flora/Fauna**: Identify non-magical plants and animals.
- **Avoid Hazards**: Can detect and avoid natural hazards (e.g., quicksand, rockslides, etc.) if actively looking for them.
- **Wilderness Awareness**: +3 on perception rolls and rolls to avoid being surprised in wilderness settings.
