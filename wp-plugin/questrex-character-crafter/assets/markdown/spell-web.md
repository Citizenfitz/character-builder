This spell conjures a mass of fibrous, sticky webbing. Creatures in the area of effect, or attempting to move through it, must make a DEX-modified saving throw or be entangled. Entangled creatures cannot move but may make a strength attribute check **(DC 20)** each minute to escape. The web is highly flammable and if set alight will burn away in two rounds inflicting 2d4 on creatures trapped within it. The material component is a bit of spider web.
