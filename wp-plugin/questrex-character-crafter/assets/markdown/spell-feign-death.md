This spell induces in a willing target a form of catalepsy indistinguishable from death. The affected creature:

- Is aware of their surrounds and can hear and smell normally
- May not see
- May not feel anything - even damage inflicted upon them!
- Is immune to asphyxiation, level draining, paralysis, aging, death effects, or hold effects
- Is immune to poison and disease as long as they remain under the spell but will suffer normally from either in their system once the spell ends.

The duration of this spell can be programmed when it is cast to automatically end early.
