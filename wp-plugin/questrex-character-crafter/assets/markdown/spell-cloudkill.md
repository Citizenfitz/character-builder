This spell summons a ghastly fog of murderous vapors. The cloud fills a 30' diameter and can move 10' per round as the caster directs. It clings to ground level and will sink to the lowest points of the terrain. High winds will disperse _Cloudkill_, but it will never flow back at the caster. Creatures within the cloud:

- Have vision limited to 3'
- Must make a Constitution-modified saving throw each round or die. Creatures with fewer than 4 hit dice or levels make this save with _disadvantage_.

_Cloudkill_ is both poisonous and highly corrosive, and affects creatures that do not breathe. It will corrode and possibly destroy items it comes in contact with (GM's discretion). The material components are a bit of lye and mustard seed.
