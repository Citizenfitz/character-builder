**Modifier:** Dexterity or Intelligence as fitting the situation  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Intelligence, Wisdom, or Dexterity. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
With a successful roll character can escape shackles, ropes, and bindings as well as devise methods of escaping prisons, cages, dungeons, etc. Escapology may be used for specific events like escaping a pair of manacles or, with the GM's approval, whole scenarios such as escaping a prison with a single successful roll.

##### Escapology Difficulty Classes

|          Action           | DC  |                         Description                         |
| :-----------------------: | :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------: |
|     Basic Restraints      | 10  |              Ropes, chains, or simple bindings              |
|    Standard Restraints    | 15  | Manacles, nets, or magical effects (e.g., _Entangle_ spell) |
|    Secure Confinement     | 20  |               Simple jail cell or locked cage               |
| High-Security Confinement | 25  |           Well-guarded dungeon or reinforced cell           |
|    Extreme Confinement    | 30  |         Heavily fortified prison with magical wards         |
