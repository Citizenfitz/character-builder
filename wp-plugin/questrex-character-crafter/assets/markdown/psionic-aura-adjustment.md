_Aura Adjustment_ allows the user to **cloak**, **alter**, or **balance** the aura of one target.

- **Cloak** - Completely hide the target's aura and alignment from all detection abilities and spells. Target may not be the subject of _Remote Viewing, Scrying_, or _Mind Stalking_.
- **Alter** - Change how a target's aura and/or alignment will register to detection abilities and spells. The target's actual aura and alignment are not changed. Objects or areas may be imbued with an aura and can then be subject to _Remote Viewing_.
- **Balance** - Allows the target an immediate saving throw to shrug off the effects of a single non-permanent instance of _Psychic Wounding_ (q.v.) or one of the following effects: paralyzation (any type), _Curse Spell, Haste Spell, Ray of Enfeeblement, Slow Spell, Suspend Life, Hold Person_. If the target fails their saving throw they may not be the target of Aura Adjustment balancing for that affliction again for 24 hours.
  _Aura Adjustment_ has a duration of one hour except when used for balancing in which case its duration is instant.
