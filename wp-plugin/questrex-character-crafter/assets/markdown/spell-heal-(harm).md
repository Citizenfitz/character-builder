The caster may touch one living creature and this potent spell will restore them by:

- Healing all hit points lost
- Curing any diseases
- Curing blindness
- Removing effects like Forget or Feeblemind spells.
  If _Heal_ is used on an undead creature it will drain them of all but 1d4 hit points.

**Reversible: Harm -** The reverse of the spell, _Harm_, drains the victim of all but 1d4 hit points and inflicts a _Cause Disease_ spell upon them.
