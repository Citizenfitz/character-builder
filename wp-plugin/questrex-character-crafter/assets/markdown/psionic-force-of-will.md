_Force of Will_ allows the user to summon psychic energies to perform extraordinary physical feats. The disciple affords the user one of the following three abilities, each with its own PSP cost:

- **Leap** for 1 PSP - make a single leap up to 30' in distance
- **Strength** for 2 PSP - Increase their strength to 19 ( or by +1 if their strength is already 19 or higher) and gain advantage on all strength roll and saving throws
- **Speed** for 5 PSP - gain Haste as the spell (but no recovery needed afterwards) for 1 round.
  A user may not use more than one _Force of Will_ ability per round.
