This spell conjures an intangible, invisible sensor through which the caster can see and hear as if they were standing in its location. The sensor appears anywhere within the spell's range the caster desires and can be moved at 30' per round thereafter. The caster may switch their senses between their body and the sensor by using an action but may not use both at the same time. Clairvoyance is stymied by a thin sheet of metal or stone more than 1' thick.
Targets with the _Arcane Sensitivity_ or _Psychic Sensitivity_ talents will automatically detect being viewed with _Clairvoyance_ on a DC 15 check (a dread sense of being watched) and may then end the spell with a WIS-based saving throw.
