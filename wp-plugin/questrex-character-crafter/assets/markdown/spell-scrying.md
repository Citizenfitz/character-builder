This spell functions exactly like the spell _Clairvoyance_ (q.v.) except that it allows a caster to see and hear a creature (not a place) over any distance and even into other planes of existence. The results are viewed on still water, a crystal, a veil of mist, etc. and may be made visible for others to see and hear.
Targets automatically detect scrying with a DC 20 perception roll (a dread sense of being watched). Targets with the _Arcane Sensitivity_ or _Psychic Sensitivity_ automatically detect scrying on a **DC 15** check with these talents and may then end the scrying with a WIS-based saving throw. Targets who have thus ended scrying are immune to scrying from the same caster for the next 24 hours.
