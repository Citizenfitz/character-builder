The character is pledged to live in accordance with and defend the natural order. Specifically, he or she:

- May never live for long periods in non-natural settings
- May never wear metal armor or carry large amounts of metal
- May never cause destruction of natural elements or creatures beyond their modest needs (such as killing for sustenance).
- Have an imperfect obligation to balance, protect, and preserve nature and animals (including humans).
- Characters with this disadvantage must always have a True Neutral alignment.
