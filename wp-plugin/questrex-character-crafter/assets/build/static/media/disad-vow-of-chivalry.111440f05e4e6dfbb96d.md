Characters with this disadvantage must always have a Lawful alignment. They adhere to a strict chivalric code and must:

- Never lie, cheat, or go back on their word.
- Must always engage in fair play in personal, one-on-one combat. This means avoiding ranged or surprise attacks, or leveraging any situation that would unfairly advantage them against their foes (personal combat is not the same a melee in a chaotic dungeon).
- Must always and everywhere be true to the ideals of the overarching cause they serve (the cause of good, evil, a crusade, etc.)
