This spell allows the caster to perform the following transference between two living creatures he or she is in contact with:

- Transfer 2d8+2 hit points, or
- Transfer a disease

Each unwilling creature is allowed a CON or WIS-modified saving throw and if either succeeds the spell fails.

- Hit points lost via _Vampiric Transfusion_ are treated as damage and will heal normally.
- Hit points transferred cannot exceed the current HP of the creature they're coming from.
- Hit points a creature gains from this spell that exceed their natural total vanish in 1 hour.
