The character may kill nonsignificant NPCs with a successful roll. Assassination performed again significant NPCs or player characters MUST always be played out thru a normal scenario (though a successful assassination roll may give the assailant some advantage at the GM's discretion). Assassination requires proximity to the victim (as close as the same town), knowledge of the victim's location, and 1d6 days (each day cut short increases the DC by 2). A failed attempt may indicate that some significant clue as to the attacker's identity has been left behind. A natural 1 roll indicates the assassin has been trapped and must fight their way out of the attempt.

| ACTION                                                | DC  |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | :-: |
| Kill easy target (bum asleep in gutter at night)      | 10  |
| Kill average target (shopkeeper, peasant)             | 15  |
| Kill hard target (minor noble)                        | 25  |
| Kill very hard target (important, well guarded noble) | 30  |
| Kill exceptionally hard target (king, emperor ,etc.)  | 35  |
