Lizard men are amphibious humanoids with reptilian heads and taloned limbs. They do not have tails. They are of dim intellect but formidable physicality. The gatherer in tribes, often near bodies of water. They are able to swim quite speedily and can hold their breath underwater for up to an hour.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Life Support** - may hold their breath for up to an hour.

### Lore

- There are underwater caverns near bodies of water filled with primitive lizardmen tribes.
