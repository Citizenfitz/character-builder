One of the more fearsome denizens of the deep deserts, the sand mantis is terrible threat to those crossing the wastelands. It's natural camouflage allows it to lurk half-buried in the sand where it can spring upon prey passing by.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Darksight**

### Lore

- There is a beautiful version that looks just like a giant flower and hides in forests
- Distant gelfs use these as flying mounts
- They are vulnerable to, and scared of lightning
