This spell may be cast as an instant Combat Reaction even if the caster has already acted in this round. This spell creates a duplicate of any one ranged spell that is 5th level or lower as it is being cast. The duplicate spell:

- Is identical to the original in effect and uses the original caster's stats for damage, duration, etc.
- Is under the control of the _Spell Fork_ caster for determining target or any other options.
