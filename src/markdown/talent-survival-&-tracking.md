The character is able to live off the land and survive in harsh environments. They can find water and food, build shelters, predict weather, and avoid being lost in the wild. With a successful roll they can track others and identify creatures by their tracks or conceal their own tracks from pursuers (as a contested roll, see Actions & Dice Rolls q.v.).

Finally, they have _advantage_ on perception rolls in wilderness settings.
