**Modifier:** Intelligence  
**Bonus:** none  
**Prerequisite:** Wizardry 1  
A character can alter _Wizardry_ spells on-the-fly with a successful talent roll, trading one parameter for another, subject to GM approval. _Spell Refashionment_ may be used once per day per level with the talent. Radical dweomer shifts (e.g., fire spell to cold spell) will increase the DC while extreme changes (e.g., _Fly_ to _Magic Missile_) are not allowed. Failed _Refashionment_ rolls cause the spell to fizzle without effect but significant failures (10 or more below DC) may yield contrary or calamitous results!

##### Refashionment Difficulty Classes

|     Action      | DC  |                             Examples                              |
| :-------------: | :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------: |
| Cosmetic Change |  5  |              Color of a _Fireball_; hue of _Light_.               |
|     Change      | 15  | Double range, halve area; increase casting time, double duration. |
|  Major Change   | 25  |        Fire spell to cold; _Wall of Ice_ to _Wall of Mud_.        |
| Extreme Change  | n/a |                     _Fireball_ to _Teleport_.                     |

**Example:** Cinder, the pyromaniacal mage, uses _Spell Refashionment_ on _Fireball_ to double its area of effect while halving its range. Unfortunately, they roll a 1 and the GM rules that the spell erupts, replete with double area of effect, at Cinder's feet...
