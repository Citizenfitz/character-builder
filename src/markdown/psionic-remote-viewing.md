This power functions exactly like the ability _Clairvoyance_ (q.v.) except that it allows a user to see and hear a creature (not a place) over any distance and even into other planes of existence. _Remote Viewing_:

- May only target creatures with an aura
- Requires a successful roll on the _Remote Sensing Table_ (q.v.) and is subject to the frequency and duration limitations of that table.
- May move the point of view no more than 120' from the original viewing location.
- Does not indicate the location of the target beyond what the user is able to see and hear.
