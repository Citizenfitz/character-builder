An absorber course is an undead created when a humanoid's aura suffers a bizarre inversion. This grants the being remarkable strength and durability but also a ravenous hunger to leech away all nearby energies. The creature's touch drains not only two life levels from living creatures but also drains energy crystals, batteries, and negates energy-based effects such as a _Mental Barrier_, _Wall of Fire_, or _Wall of Force_.

Energy attacks striking the creature do not inflict damage and instead add 1/4 of their damage to the corpse's hit point total. Thus a lightning bolt striking an absorber corpse for 40 points of damage would instead raise its hit points by 10. Any hit points exceeding the corpses normal total dissipate after 1 hour. The creature cannot absorb _Psychic Lightning_ and it inflicts damage upon it normally.

These creatures also exudes an invisible power draining globe around them for 30' distance. Within this globe all electrical or powered devices will not function and flames or artificial lighting will be dimmed almost to extinguishment. The creature can pause this effect for short durations up to an hour.

Absorber corpses appear as horribly burned humanoid with dull glowing red eyes. They retain whatever talents and knowledge they had in their former lives. Unlike many undead, absorber corpses are perfectly comfortable in full sunlight and seem to enjoy basking in it to suckle up trickles of energy.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Darksight**
- **Life Draining** - 2 levels with a successful melee hit
- **Immunity** to all mind-based effects
- **Life Support:** no need to sleep, breath, eat, immune to effects of a vacuum.
- **Energy Absorption** - Energy attacks (except _Psychic Lightning_) add 1/4 of their damage to the corpse's hit points instead of inflicting damage.
- **Energy Negation** - May negate energy-based effects such as _Wall of Force, Wall of Fire, Mental Barrier,_ etc. by touching them.
- **Energy Drain** - Drain energy crystals or batteries by touching them
- **Energy Dampen** - Dampens all energy sources within 30' making most powered artifacts useless. Also causes flames or electric lights to become extremely dim, etc.
- **Spell Ability:** Feign Dead
