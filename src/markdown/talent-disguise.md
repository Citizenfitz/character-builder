With a successful roll a character can disguise themselves. The more radically they change the way they look the more difficult the disguise roll should be. Attempts to successfully impersonate a person well known to those viewing the character will prove to be exceedingly difficult, if not impossible! A natural 1 roll indicates the character thinks they are disguised but are, in fact, obviously misrepresenting their appearance to all who view them. Characters in disguise will often present themselves in dark, misty, or distracting environments when viewers will have natural penalties to their perception rolls.
