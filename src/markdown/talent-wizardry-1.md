**Modifier:** Intelligence  
**Bonus:** none  
**Prerequisite:** None  
Characters can cast _Wizardry_ spells of any one school type (Blue, Black, Green, Red, or White). This type must be chosen when the talent is taken and does not change. See the Magic section for details (q.v.).

**Option:** In many campaigns magic is an innate talent a character is born with and cannot be learned. In such cases tell players that _Wizardry 1_ may only be taken by a character at level 1.
