Shaders are a secretive humanoid race who all naturally radiate Mind Clouding (always on, requiring no psionic roll or PSP costs). They are immune to the Mind Clouding of other shaders and can elect to pause clouding the mind of specific individuals. For those able to see them, shaders appear as humans with olive skin and deep blue hair. They commonly paint blue geometric symbols on their skin.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Talent:** Stealth
- **Psionic Ability**: Mind Clouding - Always on. Does not affect other shaders and can be paused on any creature the shader desires.

### Lore

- Shaders come from and invisible stronghold that only they can see and approach
- Shaders worship the sun or a sun god
- Shaders somehow created Gate Mastiffs
