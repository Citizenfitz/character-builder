The clutch vine is a carnivorous bush that ensnares and strangles its prey. It has a number of thorned tendrils it will lash out with to slash opponents (for 1d6 damage) or grab them (with a 15 strength) for 1-4 damage which will automatically happen each round until the target escapes. Clutch vines grow in a variety of sizes and shapes. They have brown vines tipped with deep green thorns.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- Grab attack with no attack penalty
- **Immunity** to mental attacks

### Lore

- Clutch vines can be trained to respond to basic verbal commands and guard areas
- small clutch vines can be fashioned and trained as wearable weapons
