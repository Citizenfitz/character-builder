This spell creates an apparition illusion that transforms the appearance of terrain features. Thus a barren desert could be made to look like a lush oasis or a thick copse of trees disguised as an empty glen. The illusion includes audio, visual, and olfactory elements but is completely intangible. The caster can choose one of two durations for the spell:

- 24 hours, or
- Permanent until touched by an intelligent creature.
