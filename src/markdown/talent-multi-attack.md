The character may make multiple attacks per round. The number of attacks depends on the character's level with the talent:

- **1st level = Deathstrike** - the character gains an extra melee attack whenever they strike a killing blow with a melee weapon up to a maximum of attacks equal to 1 + their level with this talent. Thus, a 4[^th] level fighter could perform 5 total melee attacks assuming her or she performed 4 killing blows in succession on their turn in combat.
- **6[^th] level** = 3 attacks every 2 rounds (3/2). The character makes an extra attack every other round (1 on odd rounds, 2 on even)
- **12[^th] level** = 2 attacks per round (2/1)
