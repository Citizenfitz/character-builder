**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
The character may make multiple attacks per round. The number of attacks depends on the character's level with the talent:

- **Deathstrike**: On a melee killing blow, gain an extra melee attack this round. If that attack is a killing blow, they gain another one and so on up to their Deathstrike total. Thus a character who is 4th level with Multi-Attack could attack 5 opponents in one round assuming their first four attacks were killing blows.
- **3 Attacks Every 2 Rounds (3/2)**: At 8th level the character makes an extra attack every other round (1 on odd rounds, 2 on even).
- **2 Attacks per Round (2/1)**: At 12th level the character attacks twice a round.

##### Multi Attack Levels

| Level | Attacks per Round | Deathstrike Total |
| :---: | :---------------: | :---------------: |
|  1st  |         1         |         2         |
|  4th  |         1         |         4         |
|  8th  |        3/2        |         6         |
| 12th  |        2/1        |         8         |
