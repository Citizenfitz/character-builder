**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** special  
**Prerequisite:** Must be 1st level. Cannot have any other race talent.  
Gnomes, distant cousins of dwarves, are similar in height but more slender in build. Standing about 4 feet tall and weighing approximately 100 lb, they often have large, bulbous noses. Gnomes possess a stronger affinity for magic than dwarves, particularly favoring illusion magic. Their personalities are typically jovial and mischievous, contrasting with their dour dwarven kin. They speak a dialect of Dwarvish.

- **Languages**: Common, Dwarvish, Kobold, the secret language of burrowing mammals.
- **Movement**: 25’.
- **Saving Throws**: +2 vs. magical effects, +4 vs. poisons.
- **Darksight**: As the spell.
- **Size**: Too small to use standard human armor, clothing, longer weapons, or longbows.
- **Perception Bonus**: +3 to all perception rolls and checks in underground settings for non-magical traps, hidden pathways and doors, new construction, or sloping passages.
