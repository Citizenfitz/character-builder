This spell divines whether a single, well-defined action taken within the next 30 minutes will be to the weal or woe of the caster. The spell has an accuracy rate of 70% plus 1% per the caster's level. This roll is made secretly by the GM and on a failure a false answer is returned. _Augury_ only informs on immediate, not long term consequences. The material component is a brew infused with a crushed pearl.
