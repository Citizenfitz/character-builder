This spell calls forth an opaque, blazing wall of magical fire. The wall lasts as long as the caster maintains concentration on it or 1 round per level if they cease concentration. The wall may either be:

- A stationary wall of up to 20 feet square per level, or
- A ring of fire that surrounds and moves with the caster with a 5ft radius per level and a height of 20 feet.

The wall inflicts:

- 4d4 +1 per level of caster to creatures passing through it
- 2d4 to creatures within 10' of the wall's outside
- 1d4 to creatures within 20' of the wall's outside
- Double damage on undead or shadow creatures

If the wall is created where a creature exists it is allowed a saving throw to remain on the side of the wall it wishes. If it fails, the caster decides which side it remains on.
