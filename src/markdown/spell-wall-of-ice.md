This spell conjures a translucent, immobile pane of thick, durable ice. The wall can be of any rough shape up to 1200 square feet in size but must be created upon a vacant, solid surface. If the wall is created where a creature exists it is allowed a saving throw to remain on the side of the wall it wishes. If it fails the caster decides which side it remains on. The material component is a bit of quartz.
