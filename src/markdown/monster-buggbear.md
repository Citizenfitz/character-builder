The buggbear, as its name would suggest, appears as a large (7ft high) bear-like humanoid with numerous insect-like features. It has great multifaceted eyes, stubby mandibles for a mouth. and chitinous plates around its mid-section. The rest of its body is covered in shaggy brown fir. Its hands and feet are clawed.

Buggbears are equally at home underground or in wilderness environments. They have been known to associate with cobols and ettins. They speak their own tongue, that of thoks, cobols, and common.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Darksight**
- **Talent:** stealth

### Lore

- Buggbears regard Insectare favorably.
- The royal jelly of the thok queen can have fantastical effects on a buggbear
- Some buggbears can administer a venomous sting with their claws or mandibles
