The spell empowers the caster to detect any falsehood spoken to them as being untrue. The material component is a bit of gold dust for _Detect Lie_ and a bit of brass dust for _Undetectable Lie_.
**_Reverse: Undetectable Lie -_** The reverse of this spell blocks the effect of Detect Lie and makes any bald-faced untruths spoken by the caster seem reasonable.
