**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** none  
Character gains an attack bonus and damage multiplier when making a successful hand-to-hand attack on an unaware opponent. Despite its name, the attack need not come from the rear, nor is it limited to piercing weapons. Any successful non-ranged attack made on an opponent who doesn't see it coming will suffice. The size of the multiplier is determined by the character's level with the talent.

##### Backstabbing Levels

| Level | Attack Bonus | Damage |
| :---: | :----------: | :----: |
|  1st  |      +2      |   x2   |
|  4th  |      +3      |   x3   |
|  8th  |      +3      |   x4   |
| 12th  |      +6      |   x4   |
