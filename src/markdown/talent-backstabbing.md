Character gains +4 to attack _(+2 for adjacent, +1 for opposing)_ and a damage multiplier when making a successful hand-to-hand attack on an unaware opponent. Despite its name the attack need not come from the rear, nor is it limited to piercing weapons. Any successful non-ranged attack made on an opponent who doesn't see it coming will suffice. The size of the multiplier is determined by the character's level with the talent.

- 1-4 level = x2 Damage
- 5-8 level = x3 Damage
- 9-12 level = x4 Damage
