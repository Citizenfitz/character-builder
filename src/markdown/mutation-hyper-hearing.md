The character makes all hearing perception rolls with advantage and may hear up to 300 ft away as if they were standing right next to the source of the sound. They may also detect the use of echolocation or other ultra/subsonic sounds within 300 ft with a successful perception roll. The character has _Vulnerability_ to sonic attacks and against them suffers:

- _Disadvantage_ on saving throws
- +1 point per die of damage
- Double the duration of any effect
