This spell may be cast as an instant Combat Reaction even if the caster has already acted in this round. _Counterspell_ allows an opponent's spell to be negated as it is being cast. To succeed, the caster must win a Contest Challenge roll of their magical talent (Wizardry or Prayers) at +3 vs their opponent's magical talent. If they win, the opponent's spell fizzles without effect. _Counterspell_ has no effect on spells that have already been cast.
