This magic compels a creature to perform a service for the caster and return with proof of its completion. A _Quest_ can be dangerous but it cannot be intentionally self-destructive to the target. Targets are allowed a WIS-modified saving throw to resist the effect. Targets delaying, disregarding, or perverting the clear intent of a _Quest_ spell suffer any number of effects at the GM's discretion such as:

- Harassment or attack by supernatural creatures
- _Disadvantage_ or Talent rolls
- A sickness that reduces one of their attributes by one point per day (e.g. hideous boils reducing Charisma, terrible nightmares reducing Constitution, etc. )

**_Reverse: Remove Quest -_** The reverse of the spell, _Remove Quest_, can nullify the effects of a _Quest_ spell on a target.
