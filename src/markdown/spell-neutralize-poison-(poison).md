_Neutralize Poison_ instantly detoxifies any venom in a creature or substance touched. It can even neutralize the poison inside a venomous creature (till such a time as it is able to recreate more). Unwilling creatures are allowed a CON-modified saving throw to resist.

**_Reverse: Poison_** **-** The reverse of the spell, _Poison_, requires the caster to touch the victim in combat. The victim is then allowed a CON-modified saving throw to resist. If they fail they die.
