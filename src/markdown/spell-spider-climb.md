_Spider Climb_ enables the creature affected to transverse vertical surfaces just like a spider. This locomotion is done at normal speed, does not require the use of their hands, and allows them to even hang upside down on ceilings. Creatures under the effect of _Spider Climb_ are immune to entanglement by the _Web_ spell and may travel along its strands at walking speed. The material component is the leg of a spider wrapped in bitumen.
