The utterance of a _Holy_ or _Unholy Word_ has tremendous effect on all creatures within 60' of the caster:

- Extra-planar creatures are forced to immediately flee to their native plane of existence.
- 3 or fewer hit dice - Killed outright
- 4-7 hit dice - Paralyzed for 10-40 minutes
- 8-11 hit dice - Stunned for 2d4 rounds
- 12+ hit dice - For 1d4 rounds they will be deafened, attack with disadvantage, and move at 75% speed.
