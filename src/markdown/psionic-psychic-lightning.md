This dramatic power casts coruscating bolts of violet-colored lightning from the user's hands at a single target no further than 60' away. _Psychic Lightning_ generates no heat or electricity instead inflicting biological corruption in the form of disfiguring sores, bleeding from orifices, and terrible pain. Targets suffer 1d6+1 points of damage .

_Psychic Lightning_ only affects living or undead creatures and is harmless to non-living targets.

- **x2 Stacking:** Psychic Lightning inflicts 2d6+2
- **x3 Stacking:** Psychic Lightning inflicts 3d6+3
