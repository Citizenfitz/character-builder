**Modifier:** Intelligence  
**Bonus:** none  
A character with this talent can perform medical tasks, such as setting broken bones or stabilizing bleeding. This talent is not cumulative; multiple characters cannot combine their efforts to restore additional hit points or grant extra saving throws.

- **Stabilize**: Prevents a character at 0 or fewer hit points from losing more hit points (see “Death’s Door” in the Conditions section).
- **Healing**: Restores damage taken within the last hour, based on the user’s level (see table below).
- **Poison Treatment**: Grants a poisoned character a second saving throw with a bonus based on the user’s level (see table). For fast-acting poisons, treatment must occur within 3 rounds; for slower poisons, it can be applied anytime before death. With a successful save, the victim recovers in 10–60 minutes.
- **Cure Disease**: Cures non-magical diseases.

##### Medical Levels

| Level | Healing  | Poison Save Bonus | Disease Recovery |
| :---: | :------: | :---------------: | :--------------: |
|  1st  |  1d4 HP  |        +0         |      1d6 d       |
|  4th  | 1d4+2 HP |        +1         | 1d6-1 d (min 1)  |
|  8th  | 1d6+2 HP |        +2         | 1d6-2 d (min 1)  |
| 12th  | 1d6+4 HP |        +3         | 1d6-3 d (min 1)  |
