The character must adhere to a strict code of honor and chivalry. Specifically, they:

- Never lie, cheat, or go back on their word.
- Must always engage in fair play in personal, one-on-one combat. This means avoiding ranged or surprise attacks, or leveraging any situation that would unfairly advantage them against their foes.
- Must always and everywhere be true to the ideals of the overarching cause they serve (the cause of good, evil, a crusade, etc.)
  Characters with this disadvantage must always have a Lawful alignment (Lawful Good, Lawful Neutral, or Lawful Evil).
