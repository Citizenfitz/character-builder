Vlux (which is both singular and plural) are a race of vile vulture/lizard men. They are man-sized but with a great span of back-feathered wings, loathsome bald vulture-like heads, stubby lizard tails, and patchy feathered and reptilian skin. Their hands and feet are cruel talons.

Vlux reek of dead carrion and possess the Pheromonic Influence mutation though all vulx are immune to this effect from each other. Vulx loath personal combat and will always seek to use their slaves, pheromones, or psychic abilities to subdue opponents. Each vulx will be attended by 1-4 slaves and 1-8 blood caws.

These creatures are highly intelligent and invariable involved in trade, particularly slave trade, wherever they exist. They wear great finery and jewels and often carry ornately decorated weapons. Despite their unsavory appearance they have a reputation as fair dealers who never use their special abilities to manipulate honest clients. Business _by the rules_ is a bedrock of their culture and personal code. Most cities have dealings with them and they have trading posts in many towns.

### Aura

Hypnotic geometric patterns of gold and purple

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Mutation**: Pheromonic influence
- **Psionics**: Domination
- **Psionics**: Suggestion
- **Psionics**: Ego Whip

### Lore

- They enjoy holding elaborate and finely appointed feasts where they consume slaves alive, delighting in both their victim's taste and suffering.
- They secretly ply in forbidden ancient artifacts
- They are highly influential in many city state governments
- They sell humans and other intelligent beings to the thoks for food.
- They are actually very peaceable creatures that intentionally cultivate a cruel persona so as to deter their enemies.
