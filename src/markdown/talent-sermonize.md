Once per day the character is able to deliver a powerful religious sermon able to stir subjects towards action. The sermonizer is able to affect up to 3 hit dice worth of creatures for every level they have with the talent and may target specific subjects or groups. For this talent to work:

- Subjects must be within 120' of the sermonizer, and able to clearly see, hear, and understand him or her.
- The sermon must be delivered for at least 10 minutes without interruption of combat or great commotion.
- The action suggested in the sermon must not be contrary to the goals of the sermonizer's religion.

The sermon affects subjects exactly the same as the spell _Suggestion._ Subjects may make saving throw modified by their affinity with the sermonizer's religion:

- Follower of religion or magical creatures aligned to the religion's sphere (i.e. an earth elemental listening to a sermon by a earth-god priest) = Save with _disadvantage._
- Follower of opposing religion or magical creatures diametrically opposed to the religion's sphere (i.e. a water elemental listening to a sermon by a fire-god priest) = Save with _advantage._
