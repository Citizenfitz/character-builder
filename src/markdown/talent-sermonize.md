**Modifier:** Charisma  
**Bonus:** none  
The character can deliver a powerful religious sermon able to stir subjects to action. This talent functions identically to Beguilement (q.v.) with the following exceptions:

- **Religious Attunement**: Suggested actions must align with the sermonizer’s faith.
- **Faith-Based Save Modifiers**: Followers of the religion or aligned magical creatures suffer a -2 penalty when saving against the sermon, whereas followers of opposed religions or opposed magical creatures gain a +2 bonus.
