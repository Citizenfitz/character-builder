This spell conjures a powerful phantasm illusion of anything the caster imagines as long as it fits within a 20 foot cube. The illusion will fool all senses, can move anywhere within the spell's range, and persists as long as the caster maintains concentration.

- **Illusory Attacks:** Creatures directly attacked (fireball, blinding light, web entanglement, avalanche, etc.) get an immediate saving throw to disbelieve. Failure indicates they react as if the attack were real and can take illusory "damage" up to 1d6 per level of caster per round. If this "damage" reduces their hit points below zero they pass out for 3d6 minutes. Upon waking or the spell's end, all illusory damage vanishes.
- **Illusory Monsters:** _Phantasmal Force_ monsters can attack and inflict "damage" that works just like that of a direct illusory attack. The monsters attack with the casters Combat Talent skill. Targets do not get a saving throw unless they have reason to doubt the monsters’ authenticity. This means the caster must be careful to have them exist and behave as they would in real life. A dragon suddenly appearing in a tavern or an orc that is immune to arrows will not be convincing illusory opponents.
