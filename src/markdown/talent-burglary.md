**Modifier:** Dexterity (locks/traps) or Intelligence (heists)  
**Bonus:** none  
With a successful roll a character can open locks and detect/defeat alarms and traps. For detecting alarms and traps, the character must specifically state they are taking time to search for them. The character can also plan larger, complex heists given 1–4 days and a successful roll grants a significant advantage such as:

- General layout of heist area and how it is guarded.
- Reduction of area's defenses (guards drawn away, door left unlocked, etc.).
- Gaining help with an inside man.
