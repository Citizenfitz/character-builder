This spell weaves a shifting pattern of colored lights into the air. Creatures within the area of effect viewing the pattern, and failing a WIS-modified saving throw become entranced and will take no action save to stare deeply into the mesmerizing lights. The spell:

- Affects number of hit dice equal to 2d4 plus the caster's level starting with the lowest HD creatures first
- Persists as long as the caster continues to weave the pattern into the air.
- Ends for any creature that is attacked or violently shaken.
- Does not affect sightless creatures.

The material components are a bit of phosphorescent material in a clear rod or a stick of burning incense.
