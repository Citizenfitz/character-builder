This power is almost identical to the magic-user spell of the same name. It enables the user to invoke a revelatory vision about any place, person, or thing of his or her choosing. To determine the result of the vision the user rolls 2d6:

- **2-5** - The user must make an INT-based saving throw (DC 17) or be afflicted with Psychic Wounding (q.v.).
- **6 - 11** - The vision is vague and grants cryptic, incomplete knowledge of the subject.
- **10-12** - The vision is clear and grants detailed knowledge of the subject.

This power is exceedingly taxing and may only be used once per day.
