This spell allows the caster to leech off hit points from another creature and transfer them to themselves. The caster must touch the target creature and the spell will not affect undead or non-living creatures.

- Caster must touch target with Combat roll
- Struck targets suffer damage equal to 1d4 hit points plus 1 additional hit point per two levels of caster (1d4+1 at 2nd level, 1d4+2 at 4th level, etc.). This damage will heal normally.
- Caster gains hit points equal to damage inflicted
- Hit points that caster gains that exceed their natural total vanish in 1 hour.
