This spell conjures a tough, pliant hedge bristling with needle-sharp thorns as long as a man's finger. The wall must be created upon a vacant, solid surface and can either be:

- 60' long, 10' high, and 5' thick or
- A circle that is 20' in diameter, 20' high, and 5' thick

The wall's dense brush blocks vision and inflicts 1d8 points of damage per two levels of caster on any creature impacting it (DEX-modified saving throw for half damage). Creatures attempting to move through the wall must make a Strength attribute check **(DC 15)** each round. Failure indicates they've become entangled and unable to move for that round. Success indicates they advance up to 2'. If the wall is created where a creature exists it is allowed a saving throw to remain on the side of the wall it wishes. If it fails, the caster decides which side it remains on.
