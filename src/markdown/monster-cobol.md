Cobols are small, subterranean-dwelling humanoids that appear as a mix of lizard and mongrel with enormous black eyes, short stubby lizard-like tails, and tiny horns on their head. They speak in a strange hissing tongue, and common. Though not particularly intelligent, cobols are exceedingly cunning. They hunt in packs using their chameleon ability to ambush passersby. They generally use only primitive weapons.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Darksight**
- **Mutation**: Chameleon Ability
- **Defect**: Impaired sense: photophobia

### Lore

- Cobols often sacrifice captives to a bizarre underground god they worship
- There are ancient cobols of high intelligence and noble purpose who seek to restore the cobol race to a greatness it once had
- Some cobols know secret underground routes below many cities and ruins.
