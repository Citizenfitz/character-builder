These 12' tall pelucidians usually appear as nothing more than ornate humanoid statues - often, but not exclusively, of a milky jade color. Originally designed for heavy labor they are monstrously strong and durable and are fitted with impressive telekinetic abilities. They are capable of levitation at slow speeds and can flash a stasis ray from their eyes to slow targets movement or their rate of falling. Finally, they have a powerful telekinetic ray that emanates from their hands and can move very heavy objects.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Spell Ability**: Feather Fall - affects up to 6 creatures. Usable thrice per day (pale violet safety ray)
- **Spell Ability**: Slow Spell - affects 3 creatures, duration of 6 rounds. Usable thrice per day (violet semi-stasis ray)
- **Psionic Ability**: **Psychokinesis** - up to X3 stacking (visible tractor ray that appears as green lighting-like tendrils)
