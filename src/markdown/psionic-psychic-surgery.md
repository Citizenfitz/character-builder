_Psychic Surgery_ allows the user to remove a single affliction from a single target with a successful Psionics talent roll. If the user fails the roll then the PSP are expended, the target is not cured, and the user may not attempt to heal that target of the same affliction for 3d6 months. The PSP cost and DC of the roll depend on the type of affliction.

| Affliction                                                                        | DC  | PSP |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-: | :-: |
| Charm Person, Fear, Confusion, Suggestion                                         |  5  |  2  |
| Non-permanent Psychic Wounds                                                      | 10  |  3  |
| Normal diseases, Poison                                                           | 15  |  5  |
| Permanent Psychic Wounds, Magically-induced diseases, Amnesia, Feeblemind, Forget | 20  | 10  |
| Geas, Quest, magical curses, Suspend Life                                         | 25  | 15  |
| Level Draining                                                                    | 30  | 30  |
