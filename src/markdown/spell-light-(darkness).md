This is a reversible spell that can create either a sphere of light or area of darkness. Each version has three uses:

- A glowing sphere of effulgence equal to torch light or an area of pitch-black darkness. Either extends for a 15' radius, and if cast upon an object it can be carried about.
- Blinding a creature with light or darkness. Creature is allowed a saving throw to resist
- Negating another light or darkness spell.
