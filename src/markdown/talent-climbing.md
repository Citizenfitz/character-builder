**Modifier:** Dexterity  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
The character is adept at scaling walls, cliffs, trees, etc., at a slow pace. A roll must be made to perform climbing in combat or adverse conditions (with the DC determined by the GM). A failure of 5 or more indicates the character has fallen from the halfway point.
