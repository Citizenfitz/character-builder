Thought slavers are an ancient and alien race of great evil. They appear as man-sized humanoids with the head of a worm with a great toothed maws and small black eyes. Their hands and feet are clawed and have only three digits. Their skin is segmented and colored a mottled purple. They are usually garbed in long flowing robes with great hoods.

A thought slaver will always have 3d6 depletion worms nestled into the segments of its flesh. These worms are under the psychic command of the slaver and can be sent out to attack enemies or perform menial tasks. The slaver can control these worms up to 1 mile away and can shift its vision to that of a single worm at will. Depletion worms attached to a slaver cannot be directly attacked or damaged.

Thought slavers exact goals are mysterious, they are most commonly out to capture intelligent creatures as fodder for their psychic composting pits. These pits are filled with loose earth and swarms of depletion worms who burrow into and incapacitate creatures tossed within. There the victims are slowly devoured as their psychic energies are leeched off for use by the slavers. The victims permanently lose one point of intelligence per day in the pit. When their INT drops to zero, they die.
