**Modifier:** Dexterity  
**Bonus:** +1 point to Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
The character excels at mounted combat and travel. Riding usually refers to horses but any number of other creatures could be substituted. It is assumed that a rider trained in one sort of steed can quickly adjust to any other given reasonable time. With this talent they may:

- **Mount Instantly**: Mount their steed as an instant action.
- **Act Without Penalty**: Perform actions like archery or spell casting whilst riding without penalty.
- **Rider Hit/Damage Bonus**: Add to combat hit and damage bonuses to attacks while mounted.
- **Charge Extra Damage**: _Replaces_ the normal +1d6 charge damage bonus with an increased amount.
- **Mount AC**: Add armor class to the mount’s base armor class.
- **Mount Speed**: Exhort steed to greater speed once per 10 minutes. Mount may maintain the increased pace for 3 rounds or 1 round per user's level with the talent - whichever is greater.
- **Special Maneuvers**: With a successful talent roll execute special actions such as avoiding being thrown from mount, having mount leap over gaps, having mount dodge traps, etc.
