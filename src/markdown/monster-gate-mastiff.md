Gate mastiffs are great dogs about the size of a black bear. They have blue fur, twin antennae, and great golden eyes. They are good natured, if fractious animals and delight in causing mischief by sneaking up on a target, grabbing them, and then abandoning them via apportation in a humorous location. Gate mastiffs are affiliated somehow with the shaders and the two are often seen working together. These dogs do not talk but seem to understand the common tongue.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Talent:** Stealth
- **Psionic Ability:** Apportation
- **Psionic Ability:** Blink
- **Psionic Ability:** Remote Viewing
- **Mutation:** Electrogenic Attack

### Lore

- The shaders use these dogs as the exclusive way to travel to and from their secret city and indeed, only the dogs know the way in and out!
- These dogs can travel to other planes of existence but very rarely do so.
- The animals can travel through time and will often show up at historically important events to observe them
