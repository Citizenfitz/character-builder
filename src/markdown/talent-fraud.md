**Modifier:** Intelligence or Charisma as is fitting  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Intelligence or Charisma. Neither can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
The character is adept at scamming, cheating, corruption, underworld networks, and general skullduggery. With a successful roll they can accomplish such feats as:

- Scam NPCs to gain advantage on reaction rolls with them.
- Forging official documents, works of art, etc.
- Fencing items, find items that are being fenced, locate black-market items to buy.
- Bribing/blackmailing officials for information, favors, etc.
- Making underworld contacts.
