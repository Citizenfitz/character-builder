This spell conjures a massive area of gale-force winds. Within the area:

- All flying creatures are dashed to the ground suffering normal falling damage and may not take flight again until the spell ends.
- Use of ranged weapons is impossible.
- Spell casting requiring gestures is impossible.
- Creatures on the ground may not move forward and must make a STR-modified saving throw to remain standing. Failure indicates they fall and tumble back 30' per round.
- Fires are put out, loose items are blown back, and less-sturdy structures such as tents are flattened. Sails and rigging on ships are destroyed.
- In deserts or other dusty areas a _Hurricane_ spell renders visibility near zero for the duration and 3d6 rounds thereafter.
