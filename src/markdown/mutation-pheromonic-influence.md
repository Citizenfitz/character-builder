The character exudes an invisible, airborne chemical that makes others compliant to their verbal commands. Targets:

- Must be within 120' of the character for at least 10 minutes.
- Are allowed a CON or WIS-modified saving throw (whichever is best) to avoid the effect.
- Failing their save they are affected as if under a _Charm Person_ spell.
- Making their save; are immune to the character's _pheromonic influence_ for a week.

Targets remaining in the 120' range are allowed additional saving throws to escape the effect as per _Charm Person_. Targets leaving the 120' range will automatically shake off the effects in four hours.
