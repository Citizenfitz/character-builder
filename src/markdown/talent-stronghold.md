This talent may only be taken by characters of 9th level or higher. The character may establish a stronghold or organization and will attract followers appropriate to their class. The type of stronghold/organization and followers will vary greatly at the GM's discretion. A knave might start a thieves guild and attract thieves. A priest might build a temple and attract acolytes and a congregation. A fighter might build a fort and attract men at arms. Below are examples with the character's charisma attribute modifying the number of followers.

| Followers               | Number                                                        |  Charisma Mod  |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------: |
| Men-at-arms             | 6d6 1st level fighters and 1d3 leaders fighters <br>level 2-5 |    +/- 1d6     |
| Thieves                 | 4d6 + 3 (levels 1-2)                                          |    +/- 1d4     |
| Congregation            | 6d10 congregants; <br>2d6 priests (levels 1-2)                | +/- 1d10 & 1d6 |
| Magic-users Apprentices | 1d6 (levels 1-3)                                              |     +/- 1      |
