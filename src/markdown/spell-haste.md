Within this spell's area of effect one creature per level of caster is sped to double their normal speed. Affected creatures:

- Armor Class and Combat Talent gain +2
- x2 movement
- Double their number of Combat attacks but NOT able to cast two spells per round.
- _Advantage_ on Dexterity-based rolls.
- _Advantage_ on initiative rolls.

When the spell ends the creatures affected will be overcome with fatigue and suffer as if under a _Slow_ spell for time equal to how long they were hasted. A _Haste_ spell negates a _Slow_ spell and vice versa. The material component is a shaving of licorice root.
