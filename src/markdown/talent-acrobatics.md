The character can perform long jumps, flips, tumbling, tight rope walking, pole vaulting, and other similar feats. A talent roll must be made to perform these feats in combat or adverse conditions. A character may avert being knocked prone or rise from a prone position instantly (zero movement) with a successful roll **(DC 5)**. The talent may also be used to lessen falling damage with a successful roll at -1 for every 10 feet they fall. If successful, they reduce their falling damage by 4d6 points of damage.

| Walk across balance beam                                        | 15  |
| Pole vault over 10' wall                                        | 20  |
| Tightrope walking, flip over top of creature of the same height | 25  |
| Tightrope walking in high winds                                 | 30  |
