**Modifier:** Dexterity  
**Bonus:** +2 points distributed in any combination to Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. None can exceed racial maximum (18 for humans).  
The character can perform long jumps, flips, tumbling, tightrope walking, pole vaulting, and other similar feats. A talent roll must be made to perform these feats in combat or adverse conditions. Jumping distances are increased by the listed bonus (L/H), and falling damage may also be reduced (see table) with a successful roll (**DC 10**, -2 per 10 feet fallen).

##### Acrobatics Levels

| Level | Fall Damage Reduction | Jump Bonus (L/H) |
| :---: | :-------------------: | :--------------: |
|  1st  |          1d6          |    +2’ / +1’     |
|  4th  |          2d6          |    +4’ / +2’     |
|  8th  |          3d6          |    +6’ / +3’     |
| 12th  |          4d6          |    +8’ / +4’     |

**L** = bonus to Long Jumps (running and standing)
**H** = bonus to High Jumps (running and standing)
