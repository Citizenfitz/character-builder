**Modifier:** Charisma  
**Bonus:** none  
By giving a performance a character can fascinate, charm, and make suggestions to nearby subjects. Musical performance is the most common application of this talent, but it could be dance, oration, storytelling, puppetry, or more. The form needs to be specified when the talent is chosen and does not change. The ability requires subjects to clearly see and hear the performance and fails in combat or amid significant commotion.

- **Range**: Affects subjects within 120 feet of the user.
- **Numbers**: Can influence up to 2 hit dice of targets per user level.
- **Targeting**: Can be directed at specific individuals or groups (e.g., only children, only rats, only Tioc the Terrible).
- **Hypnotic Performance**: A user may fascinate subjects such that they pay full attention to their performance. Fascinated viewers will follow the performer wherever they go and ignore all else as long as the performance continues. Subjects are allowed a saving throw to avoid this effect. The fascination effect is broken by the performance ending, violence, or loud distractions.
- **Charm**: Subjects under the fascination effect for 1 minute or longer can then be subjected to a charm effect from the performer that functions exactly like the _Charm Person_ spell (or Animal/Monster as applicable) and persists after the user's performance ends for 1 turn per the user's level with the talent. Subjects are allowed a saving throw to avoid this effect.
- **Suggestion**: Subjects who are under the fascination effect for 1 minute or longer can then be subjected to a _Suggestion_ spell (q.v.). Subjects are allowed a saving throw to avoid the suggestion effect.
