By giving a performance a character can fascinate, charm, and make suggestions to nearby subjects. Musical performance is the most common application of this talent, but it could be dance, oration, storytelling, puppetry, or more. The form needs to be specified when the talent is chosen and does not change. This ability does not function in combat or while a great commotion is occurring nearby. Subjects must be able to clearly see and hear the performance for it to have any effect.

#### Beguilement:

- Affects subjects in a 120’ radius of the user.
- Affects up to 2 hit dice of creatures per users' level with the talent.
- May be targeted at specific subjects or groups (only children, only rats, only Tioc the Terrible, etc.)

A character's level with the talent determines the effects they may have on subjects:

- 1[^st] level = Hypnotic Performance
- 2[^nd] level = Charm Person
- 4[^th] level = Charm Animals
- 5[^th] level = Suggestion
- 7[^th] level = Charm Monsters

#### Hypnotic Performance

With a successful talent role **(DC 10)** a user may fascinate subjects such that they pay full attention to the user's performance. Fascinated viewers will follow the performer wherever they go and ignore all else as long as the performance continues. Subjects are allowed a saving throw to avoid this effect. The fascination effect is broken by the performance ending, violence, or loud distractions.

#### Charm

Subjects under the fascination effect for 10 minutes or more can then be subjected to a charm effect from the performer that functions exactly like the spell _Charm Person_ (or Animal/Monster as is applicable) and persists after the user's performance ends for 1 turn per the user's level with the talent. Subjects are allowed another saving throw to avoid this effect.

#### Suggestion

Subjects who are under the fascination effect for 5 minutes or more can then be subjected to a _Suggestion_ spell (q.v) from the performer. Subjects are allowed a second saving throw to avoid the suggestion effect.
