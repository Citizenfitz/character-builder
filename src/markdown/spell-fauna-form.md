The spell transforms the caster and the gear they're wearing into any natural, non-magical animal no smaller than a mouse and no larger than a hippopotamus. They may only transform once with this spell but may end the metamorphosis and revert to their natural form at any time. If they are rendered unconscious or reduced to 0 hit points they instantly revert to their true form and the spell ends. In _Fauna Form_ they

- Gain the strength, armor class, locomotion, natural attacks, and senses of the creature
- May not use items transformed with them
- May not cast spells.
- Retain their normal hit points
