Grelfs are a race of plant men. They stand about four and a half feet tall, have gray-greenish skin and foliage where humans would have hair. They have delicate features, slender builds, and long pointed ears. Grelfs are known as accomplished farmers and raise all manner of exotic plants (shriekers, triffids, etc). They have resistance to plant-based poison and effects, and intelligent plants generally react favorably to them.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Resistance** - to any plant-based special effects (poison, spores, etc.) but not normal attacks from such creatures.
- **Plant Kinship** - Advantage on reaction rolls with intelligent plants.

### Lore

- Grelfs were the result of an ancient experiment attempting to allow humans to gather sustenance directly from the sun
- Some grelfs grow venomous thorns as fingernails
- Some grelfs are grown in gardens as opposed to being birthed
