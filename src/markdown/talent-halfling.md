**Modifier:** none  
**Bonus:** special  
**Prerequisite:** Must be 1st level. Cannot have any other race talent.  
Halflings are diminutive humanoids that stand a mere 3 feet tall and weigh around 60 lb. They commonly go barefoot and have large, furry feet. Their small size and natural agility makes them innately stealthy. Known for their hearty, resilient nature, halflings are highly resistant to magic, mental influence, and poisons. They typically exhibit friendly, earthy personalities and cherish camaraderie, home life, and creature comforts. This talent is unavailable to the magic-user or priest classes, and halflings may not select any magic-user talents.

- **Attributes**: -1 STR (min 3), +1 DEX (max 19).
- **Languages**: Common, Halfling.
- **Movement**: 25’.
- **Saving Throws**: +3 vs. magic, mind-influencing effects (excluding illusions), and poisons.
- **Stealth Bonus**: +3 to all stealth rolls (talent and non-talent).
- **Size**: Too small to use standard human armor or clothing; may not use weapons larger than a short sword.
- **Class Limit**: May not be magic-users or priests, nor take magic-user talents.
