Minotaurians are large bull-headed men with brutish temperaments and excellent tracking skills. They are fond of eating other sentient beings and are occasionally employed as guards or bounty hunters. One in twelve will have the psionic abilities Mind Stalking and Psi Pathing.

### Special Abilities & Limitations

- **Darksight**
- **Talent** - Survival & Tracking
- **Mutation** - Hyper smell
- **Psionics** - Mind Stalking
- **Psionics** - Psi Pathing
