The spell creates an unseen field that causes any creature entering it to be moved backwards at a speed of 30' per round or the speed at which the creature entered the field - whichever is greater. The field may either be:

- An immobile 10'x10' area directly in front of the caster
- A globe surrounding the caster at 5' that moves with them.

Note that creatures entering the field will continue to be moved backwards for the duration of the spell even if they are out if it's area of effect. _Repulsion_ only affects creatures and has no effect on missiles. The material component is a pair of small lodestones.
