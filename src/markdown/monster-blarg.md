Blargs are unfortunate looking mutants who can instantly fill their loose, drooping skin with lighter-than-air gas enabling them to float. This buoyancy coupled with their smallish wings make them kings of the sky as they are able to soar indefinitely at altitudes far beyond what most flyers can attain. Their expandable epidermises are also surprisingly tough and resistant to attacks. Most blargs are readily equipped with anchoring ropes, darts to drop from above, and warm clothing to endure high altitudes. They are often employed as scouts, lookouts, and couriers. They are known as a jovial people with a tendency for loud voices.

- **Mutation**: gasbags
- **Mutation**: Wings
- **Mutation**: natural armor (ac 5, -1 DEX rolls) - thick, fibrous skin
- **Defect**: Monstrous Appearance (-6 Charisma)
