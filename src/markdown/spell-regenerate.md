The recipient of this potent spell must be a living creature. If so, the spell:

- Regrows all lost organs or appendages
- Mends all broken bones
- Repairs any mangled limbs.

Broken bones and mangled limbs are repaired instantly. Severed body parts are made whole in 1 round if they are available for reattachment and 20-80 minutes if they need to be wholly regrown.
**_Reverse: Rot -_** The reverse of this spell causes a touched limb to instantly wither, become useless, and fall off as a festering mass in 2d4 rounds. _Rot_ requires a successful hit in Combat.
