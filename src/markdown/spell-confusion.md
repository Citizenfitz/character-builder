This enchantment befuddles creatures within range making them unable to decide on a course of action. It affects a number of creatures equal to 1d6 per 3 levels of the caster (e.g. 2d6 at 6th level, 3d6 at 9th level, etc.). Creatures with less than 3 hit dice are not allowed a saving throw. Creatures with 3 hit dice or more are allowed a WIS-modified save to resist the spell and another save every minute to shrug off its effect. Each round a creature is under a _Confusion_ spell roll a d12 to see its action:

- 1-6 - Stand confused and muttering
- 7-8 - Attack the nearest creature
- 9-10 - Attack the caster and his party
- 11-12 - Wander off for duration of spell (do not roll for this creature again)
  The material component is a drop of mercury.
