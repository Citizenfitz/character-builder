Once per encounter, and with a successful talent roll **(DC 10)**, a character can enter a single-minded, enraged state which affords them:

- +2 to hit and damage with melee weapons
- +2 to all saving throws
- +3 _(+2 for adjacent, +1 for opposing)_ to any Strength-modified Challenge rolls

While in this state they're unable to take any actions except movement and melee attacks. They will unceasingly attack targets until the berserk state ends - first opponents and then comrades!
A character may end their berserk by making a DC 10 Wisdom challenge roll or having complete solitude for 2d6 minutes. Comrades may assist in this by making a successful DC 10 Charisma challenge roll to implore the berserker to stop. If successful, the berserk character may make an additional DC 5 Wisdom challenge roll to end the berserk.
