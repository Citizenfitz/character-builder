_Creeping Doom_ summons a carpet-like horde of magical, venomous insects that fill a 20' square area. The doom can be directed to move 10' per round. It does not fly but can ascend walls and stream through small openings with ease. Creatures contacting the doom are instantly swarmed over and:

- Suffer 5d12 damage instantly
- Suffer 5d12 damage each additional round they are in contact with the doom.
- May take no action except attempt to escape the doom
- May escape the hoard on DEX-modified saving throw made with _disadvantage_
  The GM may allow creatures to escape the doom by diving into flames, water, or other similar methods.
