This hypnotic charm causes a creature to comply with a suggestion from the caster. Suicidal or obviously self destructive suggestions will be ignored unless they are very carefully worded. Creatures making a successful saving throw are unaffected but the GM can make the saving throw more difficult for particularly reasonable or convincing suggestions. _Suggestion_ does not impart any special language ability and it cannot affect undead or mindless creatures. The material component it a bit of honey or a snake's tongue.
